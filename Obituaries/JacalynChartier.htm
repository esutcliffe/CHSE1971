Jacalyn Ann Chartier (Pico)

CONFIRMED_DECEASED. Flag: exact birth/death days unknown (years only).

Jacalyn Ann Chartier of Allen Avenue was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971 (Red Cross Council; Cross Country Manager). Class records list her married name as Pico. Ed DeJesus’s April 2011 class newsletter reported that she died in 2002 and pointed to a Legacy / Sun-Sentinel online notice (full text not captured). A Star of David Memorial Gardens (Florida) grave record lists Jacalyn A. Pico, 1953–2002, with the inscription “beloved wife, mother.” Exact birth and death days are not sourced; only the years appear on the grave record. No full published obituary body was archived.

In memoriam.
