March 3, 1952 ~ November 23, 2022 (age 70)

George M. Devine, 70, of Tiverton, RI passed away peacefully at the Hope Health Hospice Center on November 23. He was born in Providence, RI. George was the son of the late George and Florence Devine and is survived by his wife of 47 years, Fatima, his daughter Christine Hawley, nieces Cheryl Smith and Suzanne Cook, close friend Cynthia Laroche, and a circle of lifelong friends.

Music was an important part of George’s life. He was a band director, music teacher, and professional musician and played in numerous bands, including an 18-piece band orchestra, rock, and soul bands. He played trumpet since the age of nine and later played guitar and drums. He also spent some time as an Elvis impersonator for a party band. George taught at schools in New Hampshire, Jamestown, Warren, and Bristol, and worked at the URI School of Music after retiring from public school teaching in 2005.

George was an active member of the Channing Memorial Church in Newport, where he often ran the sound system for services and enjoyed many friendships.

George was a kind and generous man, and a good friend to many. He could always be counted on to make those around him laugh, he was often a prankster, and usually the life of the party. He truly enjoyed entertaining others. George will be missed by many but never forgotten.

Calling hours and flowers are respectfully omitted. Funeral services are private.

There will be a public memorial service and celebration of his life in the spring of 2023, at a time and place to be announced in the future.

Arrangements by the REBELLO FUNERAL HOME, East Providence, RI.
