Tod Garfield Smith of Rocky Hill, CT, formerly of Cranston, RI, passed away on 4/4/25, aged 72 years, after a long illness.

Tod graduated from Cranston High School East in 1971. He went on to attend Berklee College of Music in Boston and the Joe Kubert School of Cartoon and Graphic Art in Dover, NJ. He became an artist of comic books and graphic novels, and penciled for DC and Marvel Comics as well as for other publishers. He worked on a number of popular titles, including Omega Men, Green Hornet, Wolverine, The Punisher, Spiderman, Vigilante and Elvira.

Tod was also a talented guitarist who worked with many local bands in both Rhode Island and Connecticut over the decades.

He is survived by his wife, Candace Kearney, his sisters Deborah Smith and Barbara Hayden, and several cousins. He leaves behind many good friends in both Connecticut and Rhode Island.

A memorial service is being planned; details will be announced at a later time.
