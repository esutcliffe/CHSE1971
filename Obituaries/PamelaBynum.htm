Pamela B. (Wilbur) Bynum

Dec 6, 1952 — Oct 17, 2025

Pamela B. (Wilbur) Bynum, 73, of Wakefield, passed away on October 17, 2025, at Roger Williams Hospital. Born in Providence on December 6, 1952, she was the daughter of the late Ernest and Mary Ann (Budlong) Wilbur.

Pam was predeceased by her husband, James "Jim" H. Bynum, who passed away in 2002. She was a graduate of Cranston East High School and was employed for many years at E.R. Smith Associates as a bookkeeper.

A spirited and warmhearted woman, Pam was a devoted New England Patriots fan and could often be found enjoying the company of friends at the Ocean Mist Restaurant or the Newport Sportsman Club. She loved being surrounded by nature and found peace in her drives to the ocean, always with classic rock music playing along the way.

She is survived by her brother Peter B. Wilbur of Exeter and several nieces and nephews. She is predeceased by her sister Patricia Gauthier.

All services will be private.
