Gail M. Santosuosso — The Providence Eye

Source: https://pvdeye.org/gail-m-santosuosso/ (April 29, 2025)

Gail M. Santosuosso, age 72, passed away on April 15, 2025. She was the beloved wife of John Santosuosso Jr. Born in Providence, she was the daughter of the late George DeMichele and Emily A. D’Aguanno.

Gail was known for her love of her pets Salvatore and Coco. She was a true social butterfly who lit up the room. Shopping and cooking and baking were her expertise, she was given the title of the family “Chef”.

She was the backbone to the family. She was the devoted mother of Christopher Santosuosso and Lauren Santosuosso and cherished grandmother of Brayden Santosuosso.

Her visitation will be held on Friday, April 25, 2025 4:00-7:00 pm at the Romano Funeral Home, 627 Union Ave, Providence. Burial will be private.
