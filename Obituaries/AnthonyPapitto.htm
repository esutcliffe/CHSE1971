Anthony C. Papitto

CONFIRMED_DECEASED. Flag: birth-year conflict (1952 vs 1953).

Anthony Carlo Papitto of Stayton Street was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971. Public cemetery records and Find A Grave document his death on September 4, 2004, and burial at Saint Ann Cemetery in Cranston (Section 43, Lot 525, Grave 3; interment September 8, 2004). Place of death is recorded as Roger Williams Medical Center, Providence; funeral arrangements were with Romano. Class memoriam sources agree on the death date. Sources differ on birth year—Find A Grave gives July 31, 1952; class dates list July 31, 1953 (same month and day). No published newspaper or funeral-home obituary text was located.

In memoriam.
