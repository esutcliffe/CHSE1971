Dates: July 9, 1953 – April 28, 2023

George F. Bagley III, age 69 of Saunderstown, passed away peacefully at home on Friday, April 28, 2023 surrounded by his loving family. He was the beloved husband of Michaela (Sullivan) Bagley for 39 years, and the son of the late George F. Bagley Jr. and Madeline (Carr) Bagley.

George grew up in Cranston and made his home in Narragansett. He retired from the Town of Narragansett, Parks & Recreation Department in 2017. He was a devoted coach for Narragansett Little-League Baseball and Raiders Flag Football. George was an avid fisherman and enjoyed his annual fishing trip with his friends “the boys”.

George was the loving father of Christopher S. Bagley (Teagan E. Steele), and Sean S. Bagley; cherished grandfather of Cecelia, Terrence, and Winnifred; and caring brother of Karen Lewis (late husband, Richard) and Patricia Burgess (husband, Eric). He is also survived by many loving nieces and nephews.

A visitation will be held on Saturday, May 6, 2023 from 10 am to 12, noon with a funeral service to follow at 12, noon in the Fagan-Quinn Funeral Home, 825 Boston Neck Rd., North Kingstown. His burial will be private at the convenience of the family.

The family would like to invite anyone attending, to write down a short synopsis of your favorite memory of George (appropriate or not) and bring it with you to add to a scrapbook. We look forward to those memories.

In lieu of flowers, memorial donations may be made to HopeHealth Hospice & Palliative Care, Attn: Philanthropy, 1085 North Main St., Providence, RI 02904. https://www.hopehealthco.org/ways-to-give/donatenow/
