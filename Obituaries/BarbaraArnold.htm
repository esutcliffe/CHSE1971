Barbara L. Arnold

1953-09-07 – 2023-05-09

Note: Nardolillo; b.1953-09-07 Cranston; Verizon manager; no explicit CHSE line.

September 7, 1953 — May 9, 2023

69, of Cranston, passed away on Tuesday, May 9, 2023 at Greenwood Rehab Center, Warwick. Born in Providence, she was the daughter of the late Preston and Dorothy (Nolan) Arnold. Barbara is survived by her loving sister Joan Arnold and her husband Michael Votolato of FL.

Barbara was a manager for Verizon for many years before retiring. She enjoyed spending her summers at Bonnet Shores Beach Club as well as playing tennis, golfing, and gardening. Barbara also loved traveling especially to San Diego, Seattle, and FL.

Visiting hours will be held on Tuesday, May 16th, from 10:00-11:30 a.m. followed by her Funeral Home Service at 11:30 a.m. in the NARDOLILLO FUNERAL HOME & Crematory, 1278 Park Avenue Cranston. Burial will be held in St. Ann Cemetery, Cranston. In lieu of flowers, memorial contributions may be made to the Barbara L. Arnold Fund, c/o RI Foundation, 1 Union Station, Providence, RI 02903.
