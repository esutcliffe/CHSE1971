Frank N. Koslow

Mar 2, 1953 — Nov 13, 2018
Chepachet

Franklin N. Koslow, 65, passed away suddenly at home Tuesday, November 13, 2018. He was the beloved husband of Kathleen A. (Canavan) Koslow. Born in Medford, MA, he was the son of the late Harold and Harriet (Wiesel) Koslow. Mr. Koslow was the owner of Eastside Painting and Restoration for 30 years. He was a Chepachet resident for the past 40 years and was an avid surfer, enjoyed cooking, art, movies and animals. In addition to his wife Kathleen, he was the father of Jesse N. Koslow of N. Scituate and Evan J. Koslow of Chepachet. He was the brother of Linda Gershon of Warwick. He is also survived by many nieces, nephews and great nieces. A Memorial Service or Gathering will be held at a later date.

Source: Quinn Funeral Home.
