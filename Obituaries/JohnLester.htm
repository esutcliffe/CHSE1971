John M. Lester

1953-05-27 – 2019-08-19

Note: b. 1953-05-27 Providence; AZ services. Guestbook confirms CHSE Class of 1971.

John "Jack" Michael Lester — Valley of the Sun Mortuary & Cemetery (Dignity Memorial)

Source: https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/chandler-az/john-lester-8826609

May 27, 1953 – August 19, 2019

John “Jack” Michael Lester is survived by his spouse (Glenna), children (Starnette, Derek, Brian, Andrew), grand children (Noah, Cole, Braelin, Aundrea, Luke, David) and his loving daughter in-laws (Michelle, Natalie, Barbara). Jack was born on May 27th, 1953 to Margaret and Raymond Lester in Providence, Rhode Island. Jack was the middle of five children including Ronnie, Kathy, Brian and Mary. We love you so much and you will be always be remembered.

Services (from page): Visitation / Funeral / Graveside Friday, August 23, 2019.

Classmate guestbook note (on same Dignity page)

"Just found out about Jack’s passing and me and many of my Cranston East classmates from 1971 are grieving. I went to school with Jack from St Paul’s School through CHSE. He was loved by everyone (excepting opposing hockey players)! He was just a great guy who had a lot of class. I’m delighted that he was blessed with a great family and grandchildren. He deserved it! May he rest in eternal peace."
