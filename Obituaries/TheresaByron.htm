Theresa Rose Byron Minett

Theresa Rose Byron — later Theresa Byron Minett — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Narragansett Boulevard in Cranston. In high school she was Chess Club (Secretary-Treasurer), Orchestra, Mixed Chorus, French Club, and Christmas Ball Committee.

She married Gary Minett in 1974; classmate Laura Jane Waite Sullivan was maid of honor. Theresa died suddenly on Palm Sunday, 1 April 2007, of a brain aneurysm — she had been playing in the bell choir at her church in Westerly, Rhode Island, that day. She is survived by her husband Gary, of Wyoming, Rhode Island.
