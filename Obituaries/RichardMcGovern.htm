Richard McGovern — Providence Journal / Legacy (published Dec 12, 2013)

McGOVERN, RICHARD J. of Coventry, passed away on December 4. He leaves behind his beloved wife, Jean Colaneri-McGovern, as well as his devoted children, son, Michael and daughter-in-law Karen of Ipswich, MA, and daughter, Charlott, also of Coventry; and grandchildren Cameron, Alexandra and Benjamin. Richard was brother to Karen Rowbottem of ME, the late Robert McGovern, Susan McGovern of VT, Daniel and Mary McGovern of Cranston. A memorial service will be held at Living Hope Christian Church, 730 S County Trail, North Kingstown, RI 02852 at 11:00 am on Friday, December 13.
