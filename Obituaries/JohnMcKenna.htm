Birth: June 11, 1953 · Death: June 19, 2026

John B. McKenna, 73, retired, of Warwick, RI, passed away unexpectedly on June 19, 2026.

The son of the late John and Mary (Tracy) McKenna, Jack stood 6 feet 5 inches tall and was affectionately known to family, friends, and colleagues as "The Big Guy."

Born in Providence and raised in Cranston, Jack attended St. Matthew's Elementary School and graduated from Cranston High School East. During high school, he worked nights and weekends at Marcello's, a local family-owned Italian restaurant. The experience inspired him to enroll at a small, new culinary program at Johnson & Wales College. The college would later become the globally recognized Johnson & Wales University. As a student, Jack stood out among his classmates, both literally and figuratively. After earning his degree, he was invited to join the school's teaching fellowship program, launching a decades-long career with the university as an educator and administrator.

He is survived by his partner, Miriam Weinstein of Warwick, daughter Sarah Argueta-McKenna and her husband Edgard, Warwick, son Jonathan McKenna of Rumford and their mother, Anita Porter, Warwick. He also leaves a sister, Mary "Auntie Bunny" McKenna, Warwick and brother, William "Bill" McKenna and his wife Margaret of Johnston and many beloved nieces, nephews, grand-nieces and grand-nephews. For the last nearly three years, Jack's most cherished role has been "Grandpa" to Cecilia "Cecie" Argueta-McKenna, Warwick.

A self-taught guitarist, Jack had a deep affection for the music of James Taylor, which he passed on to his children and granddaughter. He also taught himself to ski, cutting a memorable figure on the slopes of Killington. Whether woodworking, repairing something around the house, or preparing his "mise en place" before a meal, Jack approached every task with precision, focus, and an insistence on doing things the right way. As a trained chef, he shared his culinary gifts generously at holiday gatherings with his large extended family. Dinner did not begin until he had completed the careful carving of the turkey and ham.

Visitation will be held on Friday, June 26, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. at the Barrett & Cotter Funeral Home, 1328 Warwick Avenue, Warwick. A Celebration of Life will immediately follow.

In lieu of flowers, Jack would be honored by contributions made to the Culinary Hub of Providence (CHOP), where students from the Genesis Center's culinary training program gain hands-on experience in a real-world setting or to Community MusicWorks, Providence, a community-based organization that uses music education and performance as a vehicle to build lasting and meaningful relationships between children, families, and professional musicians.
