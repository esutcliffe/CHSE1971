Larry E. Morse

LIKELY. No date conflicts. Class/Ed-sourced; no published newspaper body captured.

Larry E. Morse of Park Forest Road was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971 (Class Council; Chess Club; Ski Club; DECA). Class dates record March 28, 1953 – April 30, 2010. Ed DeJesus’s May 3, 2010, class email reported that Larry died of cancer (diagnosed in February) and that Elliott Richman had visited him in hospice. Classmate Jon Foster later dedicated Pan-Mass Challenge rides in his memory. No quotable Providence Journal or funeral-home obituary text for the classmate was retrieved this pass.

In memoriam.
