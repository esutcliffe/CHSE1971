April 28, 1953 - October 5, 2022 (69 years old) West Warwick, Rhode Island

It is with great sadness that we announce the death of William Duggan of West Warwick, Rhode Island, born in Providence, Rhode Island, who passed away on October 5, 2022, at the age of 69, leaving to mourn family and friends.

He was predeceased by: his parents, William Duggan and Barbara Duggan (Cassidy); and his sisters, Maureen Santos and Susanne Duggan-Ball. He is survived by: his siblings, Jack Duggan, Frank Duggan, David Duggan, Kathy Santos and Beth Duggan; his brothers-in-law, Steve Ball and Mike Feeney; his nephews, Gary Santos and Steven Ball; his great-niece Rayna; and his great-nephew Lucus.

Funeral arrangement under the care of Jones-Walton-Sheridan Funeral Home. Visitation: Jones-Walton-Sheridan Funeral Home, 1895 Broad St, Cranston, RI 02905.
