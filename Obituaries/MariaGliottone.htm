Maria T. Iannotti

Maria T. Iannotti, of Cranston, passed into eternal life on March 9, 2025. She was the wife of the late Dennis Iannotti. Maria was born in Providence, RI. She resided in Cranston. She was the daughter of the late Henry Gliottone and Ann (Buroza) Gliottone. She was a nurse in Rhode Island for many years.

She is survived by her daughter Tammy Iannotti, her son Dennis Ianotti Jr., her brother Gary Iannotti, and her sister Patricia Schwartz. Maria is also survived by her dear friend Mary Bowman and her neighbor and friend Kim St. Jean.

Relative and friends are kindly invited to a Funeral Service for Maria, Saturday April 5, 2025, at 12pm in the Manning-Heffern-O'Neill Funeral Home located at 220 Cottage St., Pawtucket. Visitation will be on April 5th at 11am -12pm in the Manning-Heffern-O'Neill Funeral Home.
