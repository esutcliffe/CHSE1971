Betty Ann McElroy

CONFIRMED_DECEASED. No date conflicts.

Betty Ann McElroy of Burr Street was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971. Class memoriam records give her dates as April 23, 1954 – November 22, 1999. A Social Security death-index listing for Betty A. McElroy matches those dates (Rhode Island SSN prefix). An April 2011 class newsletter by Ed DeJesus reported that she had died in 1999. A published notice for her brother, William T. McElroy of Narragansett, names him as brother of the late Betty Ann McElroy. No standalone published funeral or newspaper obituary for Betty Ann herself was retrieved.

In memoriam.
