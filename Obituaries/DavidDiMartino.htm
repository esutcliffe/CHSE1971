David J. DiMartino

Feb 28, 1953 — Sep 19, 2026
Cranston

David J. DiMartino, 73, of Cranston, passed away Saturday, September 19, 2026, at Kent Hospital. He was the beloved husband of Mary Stuart. Born in Providence, he was the son of the late Fiore and Maria (Pinelli) DiMartino.

David spent many years as a salesman in the industrial supply industry before retiring. An accomplished musician and songwriter, he took great pleasure in tending his garden, cooking and baking, listening to music, and making yearly trips to New Hampshire. He especially enjoyed hosting family and friends at Christmas and during the annual St. Mary’s Feast. A devoted Beach Boys fan, he was also an avid reader and history buff.

Besides his wife Mary, he is survived by his daughter Kathleen Moroso and her husband Michael of Coventry; his son Nicholas DiMartino of Cranston; and his brothers Anthony DiMartino of Cranston and Michael DiMartino and his wife Janice of Cranston.

His funeral services are kindly omitted.

Source: Nardolillo Funeral Home.
