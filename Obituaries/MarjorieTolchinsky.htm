Marjorie L. Tolchinsky

CONFIRMED_DECEASED. No date conflicts.

Marjorie Lynn Tolchinsky of Concord Avenue was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971 (Leader Corps; Charisma; Athletic Committee; DECA treasurer; Mixed Chorus; Library Aid; Art Service). Class dates record July 10, 1952 – April 20, 2009. Ed DeJesus’s June 2009 class email reported that she died suddenly at the end of April 2009 while living in Altamonte Springs, Florida. Her mother Shirley Shapiro’s published March 2018 Shalom Memorial Chapel obituary names among her children “the late Marjorie Tolchinsky,” confirming that Marjorie predeceased her mother. No standalone published funeral-home obituary for Marjorie herself was located.

In memoriam.
