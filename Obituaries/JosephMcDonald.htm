May 3, 1953 ~ September 3, 2019 (age 66)

Joseph P. McDonald, 66, of North Attleboro, MA, formerly of Edgewood, died September 3, 2019 at RI Hospital. Born in Providence, a son of the late Joseph E. and Margaret M. (Nestor) McDonald. He was a sprinkler fitter for many years and manager of the service division at FSSG. Joe enjoyed spending time with his family and friends as well as fishing, golfing and riding his motorcycle. He was an avid supporter of all Boston sports teams.

He was the brother of Kevin M. and Brian W. McDonald, Kathleen A. McDermott and Nadine P. Morrissey as well as many nieces and nephews and great nieces and nephews. Calling hours Monday 4:00-7:00 PM. Followed by a Funeral Service at Jones-Walton-Sheridan Funeral Home, 1895 Broad St., Cranston, RI In lieu of flowers donations may be made to American Cancer Society, 931 Jefferson Blvd #3004, Warwick, RI 02886
