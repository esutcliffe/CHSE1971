Sheila J. Testa

Sheila J. Testa (70), peacefully passed away at her beloved home surrounded by her loving family on November 21st 2023. Born in Providence, she was the daughter of the late Jack and Lorraine (Spayde) Salera. Sheila was a 1971 graduate of Cranston East High School, where she was a majorette. She then went on to receive her Bachelor of Arts from Rhode Island College. She spent a majority of her life in Smithfield with her husband of 49 years, Anthony Testa. She was the very proud mother of Jane Testa, Michael Testa (Carrie), Lea Maroney (Edward) and Marc Testa (Jessica). She was the best Grammy to Trinity, Gage, Gia and Trent Testa, Grace and Sophia Young, Benjamin, Nina and Vincent Maroney. She was in the field of education for many years in various roles, most recently at North Scituate Elementary School. She also spent years working with adults with intellectual disabilities and enjoyed her time with them. Funeral Services are respectfully private.
