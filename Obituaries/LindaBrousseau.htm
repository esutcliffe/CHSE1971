Linda A. Brousseau (Gormley)

LIKELY. No date conflicts. Class blog is primary public source.

Linda Arlene Brousseau of Auburn Street was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971 (Hockey Club; Thunderbolt Staff). Class lists also use the married surname Gormley. Ed DeJesus’s May 2012 class blog reported that she died in California on May 3, 2012, with no further details, and offered class condolences. Class dates record the year 2012. No Rhode Island published funeral-home or newspaper obituary was located. (A 2012 Florida Linda Brousseau is a different person.)

In memoriam.
