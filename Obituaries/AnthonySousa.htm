Anthony J. Sousa

1953-03-04 – 2024-10-16

Note: EchoVita/Carpenter-Jenks; b.1953-03-04 Providence; wife Janice; no explicit CHSE line.

March 4, 1953 – October 16, 2024 (71 years old)
West Warwick, Rhode Island
Funeral arrangement under the care of Carpenter-Jenks Funeral Home & Crematory

With solemn hearts, we announce the passing of Anthony J. Sousa (West Warwick, Rhode Island), born in Providence, Rhode Island, whose presence will be deeply missed, having departed on October 16, 2024 at the age of 71.

He was predeceased by: his parents, Anthony Sousa and Gilda Sousa (Mignanelli). He is survived by: his wife Janice B. Sousa (Gardner); his daughters, Alexandra L. Sousa, Brianna L. Sousa and Tara R. Hart (Stephen); and his grandchildren, Ella and Everett Hart.

In lieu of flowers donations in Anthony's name may be made to Colby Cave Memorial Fund Every Kid Deserves a Shot.
