Susanne (Ferreira) Archambault

Susanne (Ferreira) Archambault, 73, passed away on Monday, August 10, 2026. She was the beloved wife of Richard Archambault.

Born in Pawtucket, she was the daughter of the late Thomas and Clementina (Caetano) Ferreira.

Susanne was the adoring mother of her two daughters, Nadia D. Archambault and Erin I. (Archambault) Gardner and her Husband Anthony Rainville. Most of all she loved being with her grandchildren, Daniel, Nora and AJ and her nephew Manuel. She also leaves her siblings, Diane Russo and her husband Mark and Lisa Ferreira.

Susanne worked with children all of her adult life. She was an Elementary school teacher in the Warwick School System for 17 years. She graduated with Early Childhood and Elementary Certifications, Bachelor of Science, and Master of Science degrees from the University of Rhode Island. Susanne had a passionate love of teaching and felt right at home in the classroom caring for and instructing her students. But, she always considered her most important job was raising her daughters.

She enjoyed sewing, stained glass and most of all she loved being with her husband, daughters, grandchildren, nephew, and family. Susanne was also a Professed Third Order Secular Franciscan. Her family would like to thank the wonderful care provided by Hope Health and Dr. James Butera.

Relatives and friends are invited to attend Susanne's visitation on Monday, August 17, 2026, from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. in the Barrett and Cotter Funeral Home, 1328 Warwick Avenue, Warwick, RI, followed by a Mass of Christian Burial at 11:00 a.m. in St. Peter's Church, 350 Fair Street, Warwick, RI, 02889. Burial will be in Pawtuxet Memorial Park Cemetery, Warwick, RI.
