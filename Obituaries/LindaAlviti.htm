Linda J. Alviti (1953–1991)

Linda J. Alviti was born in Providence on February 22, 1953, the daughter of Italo Alviti (1916–1988) and Elena (Mariano) Alviti, and grew up in Cranston with her brother, Richard, and her sister, Bernice.

After Cranston East, Linda went to work for the New England Telephone Co. as a telephone operator, a job she held for 16 years before retiring in 1988. She moved to Narragansett around 1979 and lived there for the rest of her life. She was a member of the Cancer Support Group at South County Hospital.

Linda died at South County Hospital on June 10, 1991, at the age of 38. She was survived by her mother, her sister, Bernice Giramma, and her brother, Richard Alviti. Her funeral was held from the Nardolillo Funeral Home in Cranston, with a Mass of Christian Burial at St. Mary Star of the Sea Church in Narragansett, and she was buried in St. Ann Cemetery in Cranston.

Source: "Linda J. Alviti," Providence Journal, June 11, 1991, p. E-04.
