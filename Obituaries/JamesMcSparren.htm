James L. McSparren

LIKELY. Flag: death-day conflict (October 1 vs October 2, 1981).

James Lawrence McSparren of Park Avenue was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971 (Football). Class memoriam sources give birth March 30, 1952, and death in October 1981. Class dates list October 2, 1981; a Social Security death-index mirror lists October 1, 1981, Rhode Island / Providence. Both days are noted rather than choosing one. No published Cranston classmate obituary text was found. (An unrelated Newport James L. McSparren, 1950–2024, is not this classmate.)
