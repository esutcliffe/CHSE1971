Richard M. Volpe — Maceroni Funeral Home

Richard M. Volpe, 61, passed away on Saturday, February 14, 2015. Born in Providence, he was a devoted son of the late Mathew and Dorothy (Lombardi) Volpe. Mr. Volpe was an Air Force Veteran. He is survived by his loving sons, Richard and Jason Volpe. He is also survived by his brothers, John and Matthew Volpe. Please join our family in celebrating the life of a wonderfully warm hearted man. He had an open heart, filled with his love for family and he will be deeply missed. Visiting hours will be held on Thursday MORNING, February 19, 2015 from 9am to10am followed by a service at 10am in the Maceroni Funeral Home, 1381 Smith Street, North Providence. Burial with Military Honors will follow in St. Ann Cemetery, Cranston.
