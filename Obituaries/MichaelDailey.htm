Michael F. “Mike” Dailey

Apr 13, 1953 — Oct 1, 2026

Michael Dailey, a beloved son, brother, and friend, passed away peacefully on October 1, 2026, at the age of 73. Born on April 13, 1953, in Warwick, Rhode Island, Michael was welcomed into the world by his loving parents, Lester and Nancy Dailey. His journey through life was marked by warmth, kindness, and a deep commitment to his family and community. Michael was a proud alumnus of Cranston High School East, where he not only excelled academically but also forged lifelong friendships. Throughout his life, his education and experiences at Cranston would serve as a foundation for the values he cherished most: integrity, perseverance, and compassion. Michael was preceded in death by his dear wife, Chris Devine Dailey, whose memory he cherished deeply. Together, they shared a life rich in love and laughter, creating a home filled with warmth and joy. Michael's legacy is carried on by his devoted siblings Pete Dailey, John Dailey, Sean Dailey, Scott Dailey, and Erin Dailey, who were the pride and joy of his life. His family was his greatest treasure, and he took immense pride in their accomplishments and happiness. In his final years, Michael was blessed with the unwavering support and care of his twin brother, Pete. The family wishes to express their heartfelt gratitude to Pete for his boundless love, care, and devotion to Michael, particularly in his later years. Pete's kindness and dedication were a source of comfort and strength for Michael and the entire family. Michael's life was a testament to the power of love, family, and friendship. He had a passion for books and believed in the power of stories to inspire and transform lives. In honor of his memory, the family kindly requests donations to be made to an organization that celebrates and promotes literacy and a love for reading. In accordance with Michael's wishes, a private funeral ceremony will be held for close family members. Though he has left this world, his spirit will forever remain in the hearts of those who were fortunate enough to know him. Michael Dailey will be remembered for his gentle smile, his generous spirit, and the profound impact he had on everyone he met.
