Debra L. McIntyre

LIKELY. Flag: death-day conflict (March 1 vs March 18, 1982).

Debra L. McIntyre of Chandler Avenue was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971 (Art Service Council; Thyrsus Club; Charisma; Thunderbolt, including Advertising Manager; Junior Talent Show). Class memoriam sources give birth December 3, 1953, and death in March 1982. Class dates list March 18, 1982; a Social Security death-index mirror lists March 1, 1982, last residence Cranston. The 2014 Nardolillo obituary for Rose F. (Procaccini) McIntyre of Cranston names “the late Debra McIntyre” among her children. Until a primary record settles the day, both March dates are noted rather than choosing one. No standalone published obituary for Debra herself was found.

In memoriam.
