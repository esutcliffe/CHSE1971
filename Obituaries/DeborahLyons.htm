Deborah Jean (Ewing) Lyons

Apr 14, 1953 — Mar 30, 2018

Deborah J. (Ewing) Lyons, 64, passed away March 30, 2018 surrounded by her loving family. She was the loving wife of James W. Lyons; beloved mother of Marc Ewing, Heather Levesque, Michael Lyons and the late Matthew Mills. Deb was also a devoted sister, niece, aunt, cousin and grandmother. She leaves eight grandchildren and two great-grandchildren. She loved her family deeply and she will be truly missed.

Funeral services are private.
