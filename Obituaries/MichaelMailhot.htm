Michael E. Mailhot

1952-09-21 – 2024-01-29

Note: Explicit CHSE Class of 1971; born Providence 1952-09-21; died Bradenton FL; master jeweler.

Michael E. Mailhot — Brown & Sons / Legacy (2024)

Michael E. Mailhot, 71, of Bradenton, FL, passed away on January 29, 2024. He was born on September 21, 1952, in Providence, RI. Michael was a retired Master Jeweler, dedicating his life to his craft. He graduated from Cranston East High School in the Class of 1971. Michael is survived by his beloved wife, Charlene Mailhot, and his children: Michael Mailhot Jr. and Elizabeth Mailhot. He also leaves behind his siblings: Edward Mailhot, Georgette Sabetta, and Gene Paul Mailhot. Michael will be fondly remembered by his grandchildren: Angelina Fonseca, Giulia Mailhot, Alex Mailhot, Dakotah Hazard, Scarlett Morris, and Maximus Morris. He was preceded in death by his parents, Ruth (Wylie) Mailhot and Edward Mailhot, and his sister, Jacquelyn Denton. A Celebration of Life is not immediately planned. The family will notify loved ones in the future when the Celebration has been scheduled. Our thoughts are with the Mailhot family during this difficult time of loss. May the memories of Michael bring comfort to all who knew and loved him. Brown & Sons Funeral Homes & Crematory 43rd Street Chapel, 604 43rd Street West, Bradenton, Florida 34209, is in charge of arrangements.
