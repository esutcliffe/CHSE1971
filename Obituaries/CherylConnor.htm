Cheryl L. (Connor) Mahoney

September 5, 1953 — January 21, 2018

of Matunuck and Naples, Florida, formerly of North Scituate, passed away Sunday evening January 21, 2018.

Cheryl was the beloved wife of Bob Mahoney, loving mother of Erin and Michael Weis of New York City, devoted daughter of Alice M. (Cooney) Connor of Warwick and the late Daniel J. Connor, best sister ever to Deborah Connor of Narragansett, Dan and Mary Ann Connor of Warwick, sister-in-law of John Mahoney and aunt of Alison and Caraline Connor. In addition to her family, Cheryl leaves many close friends.

Cheryl worked for CVS/Pharmacy for 45 years, and was a Vice President in Merchandising where she led the Beauty business for many years.

Cheryl inspired all who knew her throughout her life, especially during the six years she lived with cancer. She lived every day with grace, determination and gratitude. She left us all with a stronger appreciation of how good life is and showed us how each and every moment is a precious gift.

Her Funeral Service will be held on Friday, January 26th at 10 a.m. in the NARDOLILLO FUNERAL HOME & CREMATORY-SOUTH COUNTY CHAPEL, 1111 Boston Neck Rd., (Rt.1A) Narragansett, followed by burial at Swan Point Cemetery, Providence. VISITING HOURS are Thursday from 4 p.m. to 7 p.m.

In lieu of flowers, memorial contributions may be made to: Lung Force, 55 W. Walker Dr. - Suite 1150, Chicago, IL 60601 or RI Community Food Bank, 200 Niantic Ave., Providence, RI 02907.
