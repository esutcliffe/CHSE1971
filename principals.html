Appendix: Principals and Staff of Cranston High School East

An appendix to The History of Cranston High School East.

Our list of principals comes from yearbooks and newspapers, and it still has gaps. All dates are approximate.1

Approximate years Principal
1892–unknown A. H. Keyes of Westerly (first principal)2
1912–c. 1917 John K. Fenner
by 1927–at least 1930 Clarence W. Bosworth (later superintendent)
by 1936–at least 1940 C. Herbert Taylor (later superintendent)
1940s–early 1950s Not yet identified
c. 1954–55 Maurice S. Bagby
by 1957–1963 Dr. Knute G. Larson
1963–at least 1972 William J. Kutneski (named August 1963)3
1972–1986 Joseph Ventetuolo (acting principal from October 1972, then principal; retired June 1986)3
1986–1996 Dr. William L. Paolino (new principal in the 1986–87 school year)3
1996–at least 2012 Don Frederick (Rhode Island Principal of the Year, 2002)
by 2015–at least 2017 Sean Kelly (start year not yet confirmed)3
c. 2017–2024 Unconfirmed
2024–present Thomas Barbieri

Many others shaped the school over the decades. Raymond R. Thompson was sub-principal and assistant principal from 1926 to 1936 and athletic director from 1936 to 1957. Vera L. Milliken was dean of girls from 1928 to 1957. Vice-principal Edward P. Sawin had retired by 1960, and Frederic B. Etter was athletic director that year.4
