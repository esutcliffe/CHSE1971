Friday, August 28, 2026

Chelo's, Cranston, RI

The 55th reunion was held Friday, August 28, 2026 at Chelo's in Cranston, RI.

That same day, about 15 classmates took a CHSE building tour (shared by Ernie Rheaume; photos thanks to Gail). The group started in the school store and toured until about 4:00 PM. Thanks to Lori and Mr. Barbieri for arranging it, and to the teacher who led the tour.

Photos from the gathering at Chelo’s were shared by Jeanne Rheaume.
