25th year reunion (1996)

The Westin Hotel

Can you contribute any memories?

Jon Foster recalls that this reunion was at the Westin and was a very good event. He especially recalls seeing Bill Dube', Pat McMullen from Chicago, George Alpine and Rhonda Port.

Pat Dewey Giarrusso thinks that a second set of permanent nametags were made for this reunion (the first batch was done for the 20th).
