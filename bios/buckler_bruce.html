Bruce R. Buckler

Bruce Buckler grew up in Cranston, the son of Samuel and Laura (Kagan) Buckler. He is listed with the Class of 1971 in the yearbook among the seniors who were not photographed, so no portrait or activities were printed for him. His sister, Barbara (Buckler) Shapiro, was also a member of the class, and in July 1975 Bruce was an usher at her wedding.

As an adult Bruce has made his home in Cranston. His parents ran a family business together: his father, Samuel Buckler, a World War II Army Air Forces veteran, co-founded United Printing and worked there for 35 years, and his mother, Laura, worked alongside him for more than 30 years; both retired in 1986. Laura died in 1999, and Samuel died in 2023 at 99. The class has lost touch with Bruce. If you know how to reach him, please let us know.
