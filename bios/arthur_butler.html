Arthur W. “Butch” Butler

Arthur W. “Butch” Butler — also Arthur “Butch” Butler III — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Netherlands Avenue in Cranston. In high school he was Swim Team, Social Committee, A Cappella Choir, Mixed Chorus, Chess Club, Thyrsus Club, and Science Seminar at the University of Rhode Island.

The class site keeps a short bio noting life among Colorado’s snow-covered mountains (site spelling *mountians* — Soft: date-stamp; Soft Buena Vista / Chaffee nonprofit & consulting trails support the mountain bio).
