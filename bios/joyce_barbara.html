Barbara Jane Joyce

Barbara Joyce grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she was a member of the Ski Club for three years, from sophomore through senior year, and of the Photography Club as a senior. She worked on The Individualist as a sophomore and on Charisma as a senior. As a junior she was a tutor and was part of Art Service.

She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Barbara, please let us know.
