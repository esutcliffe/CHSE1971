Alan Mittleman

Alan Mittleman graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. After Brandeis (B.A. magna cum laude) and a Temple University Ph.D. (with distinction), he taught religion at Muhlenberg College — including as department head — and later joined the Jewish Theological Seminary as professor of Jewish philosophy. He is now JTS’s Aaron Rabinowitz and Simon H. Rifkind Emeritus Professor of Jewish Philosophy, and a Conservative rabbi (Rabbinical Assembly).

His books run from Between Kant and Kabbalah through Hope in a Democratic Age (Oxford, 2009) and A Short History of Jewish Ethics (2012) to the 2023 Cambridge volume Absurdity and Meaning in Contemporary Philosophy and Jewish Thought. In the early 2010s he also directed the Tikvah Institute for Jewish Thought at JTS. He has credited CHSE English teacher Mr. Horton as the strongest influence on his intellectual formation.

He and his late wife Patricia (Patti) Mittleman, longtime director of Muhlenberg College Hillel, raised two sons, Aryeh (Ari) and Joel. His mother, Shirley L. Mittleman, died in June 2010; Patti died in 2018.
