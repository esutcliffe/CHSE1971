Donald “Don” Tassone

Donald “Don” Tassone graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. (No senior portrait or address/activities block in the slim yearbook file.)

He is the Providence-area songwriter who carried The Mediator Stage — first as a rotating host with Art Toegemann and Frank Martyn, then as longtime host and “music minister,” inventing the Four Corners songwriter showcase and steering pandemic-era renovations at 50 Rounds Avenue (Motif, December 2021). Class notes paired him with Art in *American Songwriter* coverage and as a musical buddy of Tod Smith from “way back.” Soft hard rule: do **not** merge with the Ohio fiction/short-story writer Don Tassone (Loveland / dontassone.com).
