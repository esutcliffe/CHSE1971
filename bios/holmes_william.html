William Francis “Bill” Holmes

Bill Holmes grew up in Cranston, the son of Herbert F. “Herb” and Frances M. (Neve) Holmes. His father, who lived in Cranston for 48 years, was a longtime leader of the Rhode Island Carpenters Union, Local 94. He joined as an apprentice in 1955, became business agent in 1967 and served as business manager from 1981 to 1993. At Cranston East Bill was both an athlete and a musician. He wrestled as a sophomore, junior and senior and belonged to the Wrestling Club all three years. He played football and joined the Football Club as a sophomore, played baseball as a sophomore and a senior, and as a senior played soccer and joined the Soccer Club. He also played in the Band and the Stage Band all three years. By 2000 Bill was living in Warwick.

Bill has stayed in touch with the class, and he has often been photographed with classmate Gene Blair. In September 2003 he came to the 32nd reunion, the class's “5-0 Birthday Party,” at the Hotel Viking in Newport, where he was photographed with Gene and Joanne Blair. He was at the 35th reunion at Yawgoo on September 9, 2006, and he was among the classmates who sent in their money early for the 40th reunion in 2011. At the 50th reunion, held on June 25, 2022, at the Bonnet Shores Beach Club, he was photographed again with Gene.

Source for family details: Herbert F. “Herb” Holmes obituary, Providence Journal, February 20, 2000, p. B-11.
