Mark Benjamin Hall, Class of 1971

Mark Benjamin Hall grew up in Cranston, the son of Frederick W. Hall Sr. (1928–2016), who worked in electric motors and controls and later founded his own firm in New Jersey, and Edith (Sinapi) Hall (c. 1927–2022), an accomplished pianist and St. Mary's Church choir member. He is one of seven children, with siblings Joyce, Lois, Frederick Jr., Jeffrey, Charles, and Judith.

At Cranston High School East, Mark belonged to the Drafting Club as a sophomore, swam for three years, and joined the Gymnastics Club as a senior.

After high school, Mark attended Berklee College of Music and went to work for Burton Industries, the electric motors and controls company where his father also worked. On May 26, 1979, he married Martha Ann Clapp of Barrington at her parents' home in Barrington, and the couple made their home in Providence. In May 1981 he served as best man at his sister Lois's wedding in Cranston. By 2016, Mark and Martha were living in Boonton, New Jersey, near the family's electric motor drives and controls firm, F.W. Hall Company, Inc., in Pompton Plains.

Mark has stayed in touch with the class. He came to our 32nd reunion in September 2003, where he was photographed with Jacob Adler and Alan Mittleman, attended the 35th reunion at Yawgoo Bakes in September 2006, and registered for our 40th reunion in 2011.
