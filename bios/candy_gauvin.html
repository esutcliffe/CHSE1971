Candace (Williams) Gauvin

Candace Elona Williams — known as Candy (Williams) Gauvin — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Potter Street in Cranston. In high school she was in Mixed Chorus.

She and Ken Gauvin, whom she had known since Park View, married, returned to finish their diplomas, and raised two sons, Kenn and Jason, in Cranston. On the class site she wrote of customer-service work at AAA Southern New England after years at the Providence Journal, church singing, gardening, and tracing Mayflower and Roger Williams family lines. Classmates saw her at the 31st and 32nd reunions. Later class notes marked a Colorado daughter-in-law’s veterinary-assistant boards, class 60th-birthday party photos she shared, and her mother Elsie’s 105th birthday and Roger Williams family books. As of the Roger Williams Family Association’s 2026–27 officers list she serves as Corresponding Secretary from Cranston.

Soft family note: husband Ken died in 2021. Do not confuse with classmate Eric Gauvin.
