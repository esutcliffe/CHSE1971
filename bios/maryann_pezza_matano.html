Maryann L. Pezza Matano

Maryann L. Pezza — later Maryann Pezza Matano / Maryann Matano — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Burlingame Road in Cranston. In high school she was Leader Corps, Wrestling Manager (sophomore through senior), Wrestling Club, Thunderbolt Staff, F.A.C. Representative, and Football Manager.

Classmates still meet her in reunion photos — with Gail Glen Whitfield at the 32nd, named in the 35th attendance list, and repeatedly at the 50th with Pat Dewey Giarrusso, Jerry Renza, Barbara Haggis Thompson, Lauren Goding Johnson, and others — and Ed’s 32nd write-up thanks her with Pat Dewey for “about a million” reunion phone calls.
