Helen M. (Desmond) McDonald

Helen M. Desmond graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She was secretary of the Red Cross Council, a homeroom representative, and a reporter for the Cranstonian East, and she worked on the Thunderbolt. She was also in the Thyrsus, Spanish, Ski, Track, and Hockey Clubs and the Folk Workshop.

Helen is one of nine children of Edward F. and Mary Rose Desmond. Her husband is Brian.

For about forty years she was Executive Director of the Rhode Island Bar Association, a post she had held for some thirty years by 2012 (Rhode Island Lawyers Weekly). She retired around 2022, and in his president’s message a Bar Association president called her “the legendary Executive Director.”

She joined classmates at the 2003 reunion.
