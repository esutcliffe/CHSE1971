Juliana Maria (Pastine) Reilly

Juliana Pastine is the daughter of Armando and Etta Pastine, who ran Armando’s Restaurant. At East she was president of DECA and treasurer of the G.A.A. She was also in the Leader Corps, the Wrestling Club, the Football Follies, intramurals, the Social Committee and the library aides.

As Juliana Pastine Reilly she has stayed close to the class. She worked behind the scenes on the 35th and 40th reunions and joined classmates at the 55th in August 2026.
