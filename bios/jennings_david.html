David John Jennings

David Jennings grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East he played both varsity and JV hockey as a sophomore, and as a senior he played baseball and was a member of DECA. David came to the class's 32nd reunion, the "5-O Birthday Party" on September 13, 2003, where he was photographed with Donna Jennings. The class has since lost touch with him. If you know how to reach David, please let us know.
