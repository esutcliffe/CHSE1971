April Irene (Amiot) Howell

April Amiot grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she was a member of Charisma as a sophomore, and as a senior she was in DECA, Data Processing and Office Practice. Class records list her married name as Howell. She is one of the classmates the reunion committee has been trying to find since at least 2011. If you know how to reach April, please let us know.
