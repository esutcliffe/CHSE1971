Dianne (Centofante) Miller, Class of 1971

Dianne Rose Centofante, now Dianne (Centofante) Miller, grew up in Cranston and graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971.

At Cranston High School East, Dianne was a Student Council delegate and served on the Class Council all three years. She was on the Social Committee and the Citizenship Committee throughout high school, and as a senior she was the school’s representative to the Superintendent of Schools Youth Council. That year she also joined the Leader Corps, took part in Cranston Follies and played intramurals. In the Cranstonian’s Senior Superlatives she was named Most Versatile, together with Ronald Stabile.

Dianne has stayed in touch with the class. She helped plan the 32nd reunion — the calls, the decisions, the decorations and the band — and she appears in photographs from that weekend. She was at the class’s 60th birthday party in 2013. She may be the Dianne Miller who has been associated with Dartmouth-Hitchcock in the Upper Valley of New Hampshire.
