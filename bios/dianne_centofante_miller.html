Dianne Centofante Miller

Dianne Rose Centofante — now Dianne Centofante Miller — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Randall Street in Cranston. In high school she was Student Council delegate, Class Council, School Representative to the Superintendent of Schools Youth Council, Social Committee, Citizenship Committee, Leader Corps, Cranston Follies, and Intramurals. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Most Versatile (with Ronald Stabile); the Superlatives page printed Diane — prefer slim/yearbook Dianne in display.

She helped plan the 32nd reunion (calls, decisions, decorations, band) and appears in captioned adult reunion photos with classmates.
