Walter Horridge

Walter Horridge grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East he was a distance runner: three years of cross country and indoor track, two years of outdoor track, and two years in the Track Club, which he led as president in his senior year. He also played junior varsity baseball as a sophomore and joined the Thyrsus Club as a senior.

Walter came to the class's 32nd reunion, the "5-O Birthday Party" on September 13, 2003, where he was photographed with David and Lisa Deverell and with Martha Robitaille Galeota and Chris Galeota, and he had his check in early for the 40th reunion at the Bonnet Shores Beach Club in Narragansett on June 10, 2011. That same month the Warwick Beacon pictured Walter Horridge of Warwick working dispatch at Mr. Messenger, the Rhode Island delivery company, after a propane explosion damaged its warehouse. The paper said he had started there 38 years earlier, working for the late Frank Lanza, who founded the company in the 1960s.
