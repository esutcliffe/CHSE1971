Marilyn Joan Wexler Kingsbury

Marilyn Joan Wexler — later Marilyn Wexler Kingsbury — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Western Promenade in Cranston. In high school she was French Club, Junior Alliance Francais, Future Teachers, Thyrsus Club, Thunderbolt Homeroom Representative, Cranstonian Advertising Staff, Debating Team, and Foster Parents.

Class notes from April 2011 sketch two college-age sons (one a Muhlenberg senior then headed toward graduate work near Tufts; the other a William & Mary sophomore) and a home near Tufts with her husband. Soft bereavement: mother Jeanne Wexler of Cranston died 18 January 2022; that notice places Marilyn and Steven Kingsbury in Marblehead, Massachusetts, and names grandsons Adam and Ian Kingsbury. Soft: date-stamp the 2011 college details — sons are adults in the 2022 notice. Exact birth date not found.
