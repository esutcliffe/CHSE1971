Paul Thomas Verduchi, Class of 1971

Paul Thomas Verduchi grew up in Cranston, the son of Anacleto S. Verduchi and Ann M. (Moretti) Verduchi (1930–2020), who worked at North Safety Products in Cranston for 44 years. His uncle, Anthony Moretti, Ann's brother, was Cranston's police chief from 1976 to 1983.

At Cranston High School East, Paul belonged to the Engineering and Drafting Club as a senior.

After high school Paul went to Roger Williams University, where he majored in architectural construction and also took some programming courses. He got his first computer, an Apple II, in the 1970s, and computers became his career. He worked in quality control for several high-tech companies in San Diego before he and his wife, Michele, came home to Rhode Island in 1990 with their son.

By 1997 Paul and Michele were living in East Greenwich, and Paul, then 44, was the editor and publisher of RAM Chowder, Rhode Island's computing magazine. He has also run Diskenchantment, a computer business in East Greenwich. The family later lived in Warwick and then in Cranston again, and Paul and Michele now make their home in Warwick.

Paul has been a good friend to the class. He and Michele came to our 32nd reunion in 2003, and in 2013 he did a great deal of the work for the class's 60th-birthday party in Cranston, including the invitations, the venue, and a last-minute DJ.
