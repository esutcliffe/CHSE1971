Helen Ruth Dittelman

Helen Ruth Dittelman — now Helen Dittelman-Dolan — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Shirley Boulevard in Cranston. In high school she was Assembly co-chair, Citizenship Committee, Class Council vice-president, Public Address System chair, Social Committee, Thyrsus Club, Hockey Club, Mixed Chorus, and Future Teachers. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Most Humorous (with Garry McDonough).

On the class site she still answers in her own voice: humor intact; banking collections gave way to alumni/development work and later college counseling. She appears in 32nd-reunion photos and was thanked among 35th-reunion helpers. Soft, dated: married to William Dolan; family bereavements include brother Lewis (2014) and sister Irene (October 2025); as of Irene’s notice they were of Plymouth, Massachusetts. Exact birth date not found.

Living / in touch as of late 2025.
