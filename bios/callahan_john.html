John William Callahan, Class of 1971

John William Callahan grew up in Cranston, the son of the late John G. “Jack” and Carmella (Travisano) Callahan. At Cranston High School East he played basketball as a sophomore.

On September 5, 1976, John married Beverly Pyles of Maryland, who had graduated from Crossland High School in Temple Hills. The young couple started out in Cranston, and they raised three children: John III, Lisa Marie and Christopher.

In 1984 the family made the move to Winchendon, Massachusetts, and that same year John opened Toy Town Tax Service, taking its name from the town’s old nickname. He has been preparing tax returns, keeping books and running payrolls for his neighbors ever since, and his clients still write about “John and crew.” The business became a family affair when his son, John W. Callahan III, joined his father’s firm. The Callahans spent the years from 1988 to 2006 in nearby Ashburnham before settling back in Winchendon, where John lives today.

John and Beverly were married for 42 years. She died in December 2018, leaving John, their three children, and five grandchildren.

John has stayed close to his classmates. For the 50th reunion in June 2021, he organized the class golf outing on the Friday of reunion weekend, and he was there for the celebration that followed.
