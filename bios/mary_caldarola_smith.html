Mary Ann (Caldarola) Smith

Mary Ann Caldarola — known as Mary (Caldarola) Smith — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Wales Street in Cranston (same house as sister Beverly). In high school she was National Honor Society, Class Council Secretary, Cheerleading, Pep Squad, Spanish Club and Cervantes Society, Wrestling Club, Charisma, and Social Committee.

Classmates following Ed’s updates knew her as a Pennsylvania first-grade teacher (30th year as of an October 2009 note) who kept running — Marine Corps Marathon 10K, a planned Orlando half, a Philadelphia ten-miler (May 2012) — and, after retirement, tennis plus an 8.8-mile race and another Marine Corps 10K with sister Beverly for the Travis Manion Foundation (November 2013). Soft, dated: kids both in college by late 2011. Do not confuse with sister Beverly Ann (Caldarola) McFarland, a separate Class of 1971 classmate.

Living / in touch as of last class data. Exact birth date not found.
