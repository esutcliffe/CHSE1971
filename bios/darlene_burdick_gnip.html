Darlene Gail Burdick Gnip

Darlene Gail Burdick — later Darlene Burdick Gnip — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Cliffdale Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was Charisma and Office Practice Secretary to the English Department.

January 2012 class notes sketch a Marine Corps start after leaving Rhode Island, marriage in 1974 to a fellow Marine (together since 1972), Florida then Maryland years, mostly government jobs plus about ten years as a daycare mom, and USPS mail delivery as of that note — with four children and two grandchildren (ages as of 2012; date-stamp; no names).
