Kathleen Anne (Canavan) Koslow

Kathy Canavan grew up on Forest Avenue in Cranston. At East she served on the Red Cross Council as a sophomore, and as a senior she was in Leader Corps and was a Thunderbolt Homeroom Alternate. She married classmate Frank Koslow (CHSE '71), and the two made their home in Chepachet, in the town of Glocester, for some forty years. Frank owned Eastside Painting and Restoration. Kathy and Frank came to the class's 32nd reunion in 2003 together.

Frank died suddenly in November 2018. Kathy's mother, Lillian (Cole) Canavan, was an accountant at Amica Insurance for twenty years and died in Cranston in 2010.
