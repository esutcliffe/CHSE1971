Michael F. Dailey

Michael F. Dailey graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Essex Street in Cranston with his brother Peter. In high school he wrestled (and was in Wrestling Club) sophomore through senior year, and played soccer (and Soccer Club) as a senior — the same activities as Peter.

He is marked in touch on the class site. Public directories place him in the Deposit, New York area and show prior California addresses in Hermosa Beach and Three Rivers; both New York and California still appear in recent listings, so this sketch names both without choosing which is current. No public career trail for this identity was found.
