Louis Alan Shore

Louis Alan Shore graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. The yearbook lists his home on Netherlands Avenue in Cranston. In high school he was named to Boys’ State as a junior and served as Governor there; he was in Chess Club sophomore through senior year (president as a senior), Track Club sophomore through senior year, and Soccer Club junior and senior years. He was still on the class missing list in March 2011.

His path after high school is not yet firmly documented for the class site. Soft adult leads elsewhere have not been confirmed as the same classmate.

Classmates are still hoping to reconnect. He is not listed among deceased classmates.

Living track; class card kept as missing.
