Florence Cardogno Voccola

Florence Cardogno — later Florence Cardogno Voccola (reunion captions often spell **Vocolla**) — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Larch Street in Cranston. In high school she was Senior Class Council and Thunderbolt Homeroom Representative.

Classmates see her throughout 32nd-reunion photos — solo portrait and groups with Cosima Fallon, Susan Rotella Vinacco, Sherry Halverson Cirelli, Dale Waterman Bonanno, Nancy Goldis Langford, and Paul Varadian.
