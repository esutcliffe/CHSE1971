Rhonda L. (Shapiro) Fallk, Class of 1971

Rhonda Shapiro grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Henry and Bernice (Dressler) Shapiro, who later lived in Warwick. At Cranston East she belonged to Future Teachers as a junior and senior, the Spanish Club as a senior, and the Thyrsus Club as a junior and senior.

She earned a B.A. in psychology from Roger Williams University, an M.A. in counseling with a concentration in gerontology from Framingham State University, and a Ph.D. in psychology with an emphasis in counseling from William Lyon University in San Diego. While completing her doctorate in California she met David Fallk; the couple later settled in the Scranton area of northeastern Pennsylvania and made their home in Clarks Summit. They have two children, Rebecca Sarah (“Becky”) Fallk and Murray Stuart Fallk.

Before Scranton, Rhonda worked as a counselor and mental-health rehabilitation worker for the state of Rhode Island and as a counselor and activities advisor for older adults at Cushing Hospital in Framingham, Massachusetts. In Scranton she served as a social worker and as coordinator of the Kosher Meals on Wheels program for Jewish Family Service. For many years she co-facilitated an Alzheimer’s Association caregiver support group at Elan Gardens in South Abington Township. In 2018 she and David were named co-chairs of the Jewish Federation of Northeastern Pennsylvania’s 2019 UJA Campaign.

In September 2019 she launched Cafe Connections, a memory café for people with memory loss and their caregivers, as a pilot program at the Scranton Jewish Community Center. As of March 2024 she was still facilitating Cafe Connections there, designing activities and welcoming participants with the help of trained volunteers, with grant support from the Lackawanna County Agency on Aging.

Rhonda remains on the class’s list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach her, please let us know.
