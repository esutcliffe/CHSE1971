Ray Jane (Eisenstadt) Immerman

Ray Eisenstadt grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she was one of the class's leaders: treasurer of the Class Council as a sophomore and junior, a member of the Dean's Council for three years, and a member of the National Honor Society and Leader Corps as a junior and senior. She also belonged to the Thyrsus Club as a sophomore and junior and the A.V.A. as a sophomore, and as a junior she took part in the University of Rhode Island's Seminars in Oceanography. Class records list her married name as Immerman. Ray has been on the class's list of missing classmates since at least 2011, and we would love to hear from her. If you know how to reach Ray, please let us know.
