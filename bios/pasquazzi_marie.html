Marie Susan (Pasquazzi) Lawton

Marie Pasquazzi grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Americo and Antonette "Etta" (Morgera) Pasquazzi. Her mother, who was born in Providence, lived most of her life in Cranston and devoted herself to her family before moving to Coventry in 1998. At Cranston East Marie was a member of the National Honor Society and the Cervantes Society in her junior and senior years. As a sophomore she belonged to the Italian Club and did tutoring, and as a senior she joined the Spanish Club.

As an adult Marie has gone by Maria S. Lawton, and by 2005 she had made her home in Coventry, Rhode Island. She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Marie, please let us know.
