Elaine Marie (Fox) Miele, Class of 1971

Elaine Marie Fox grew up in Cranston, one of the daughters of Gerhardt and Viola (Brindell) Fox. At Cranston High School East she was a member of Future Teachers of America for three years and also took part in Folk Workshop, Ski Club, intramurals, Italian Club and Mixed Chorus.

By 2004, Elaine and her husband, Robert A. Miele, were living in North Providence. They had been married 28 years when Robert died that February at age 54. His obituary named Elaine and a daughter among his survivors.

Elaine is currently on the class's list of missing classmates. We would be glad to hear from her, or from anyone who can help us reconnect.
