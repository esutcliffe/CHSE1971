Patti Ann (Nulman) Simpson

Patti Nulman grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Larry and Shirley Nulman. Her father owned Barnes’ Cleansers in Pawtucket for 41 years, and her parents lived in Cranston for about sixty years. At Cranston East she was in Future Teachers for three years and took part in the Citizenship Committee, the Ways and Means Committee, the Italian Club and the Spanish Club, and she worked as a library aide.

Patti married Noel Simpson, and the couple make their home in Narragansett. She was at the class’s 32nd reunion in 2003, and she and Noel came to the 40th reunion at the Bonnet Shores Beach Club in 2011.
