Jakki (Kougasian) Horan

Joan Alison Kougasian — known as Jakki (Kougasian) Horan — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Poplar Circle in Cranston. In high school she was Thyrsus Club, Thunderbolt business manager, Ecology Action secretary, Charisma, Folk Workshop and Folk Show, Future Teachers of America, and Student Tutoring.

She helped the 2003 32nd-reunion phone tree finding missing classmates. Class notes in 2011–2013 celebrated her three daughters — Maggie’s American University graduation and forensic-accounting work in Virginia; Victoria’s Massachusetts and New Hampshire bar passage while working full-time through evening law school (and Jakki’s “instant grandmother” joy via Victoria and George’s children); and Corie’s Early Childhood associate degree from Middlesex Community College — and noted that she and Ed DeJesus, both Boston-area technical writers, swapped job leads. She and her husband Neal have lived in the Chelmsford, Massachusetts area.

Soft family notes (dated): mother Betty Kougasian died April 2008; brother Peter Mark Kougasian died in 2021 (not Class of 1971).
