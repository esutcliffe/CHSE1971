Robert R. Davies, Class of 1971

Robert R. Davies grew up in Cranston, the son of Stanley A. Davies and Patricia Ann (Rogers) Davies. His mother was a Cranston High graduate herself. His father served with the Cranston Fire Department from 1952 to 1973 and then joined the State Fire Marshal's Office, where he was Chief of Technical Services from 1981 to 1998.

In January 1974 Robert married Norma J. Sweeney of North Smithfield at St. Theresa's Shrine in Nasonville, and the couple settled in Cranston.

In 1976, at 23, Robert was appointed a probationary patrolman in the Cranston Police Department, and by 1990 he was a Cranston detective. After he retired from the department, he worked as a court officer in Providence.

Robert lost his sister, Cheryl ("Cherry") Onanian, and in 2025 both of his parents: his mother in June and his father in July. He now lives in Chepachet, Rhode Island.
