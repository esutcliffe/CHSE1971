Elliot Travis Richman

Elliot Richman grew up in Cranston, the son of Julius “Rich” and Jeannette (Travis) Richman; his middle name comes from his mother’s family. His father founded and for many years ran Albert’s Home Supply in Providence, retiring in 1979, and his mother managed the Brown University Credit Union for many years. At Cranston East Elliot played in the Band as a sophomore, junior and senior and in the Dance Band as a senior, and as a senior he also belonged to the Chess Club and the Spanish Club. Elliott (his name usually appears with two t’s in later records) graduated from Bryant College. On April 20, 1986, he married Phyllis Rae Krantzman in Arlington, Virginia; at the time he was with the Stop & Shop Companies and the couple lived in Boston. They now live in Milton, Massachusetts.

Elliott has stayed in touch with the class. On September 13, 2003, he came to the 32nd reunion, the class’s “5-0 Birthday Party,” at the Hotel Viking in Newport, where he was photographed with Gail Glen Whitfield. He was at the 35th reunion at Yawgoo on September 9, 2006, too; Ed DeJesus wrote afterward that “Elliott Richman and I observed that this is a really nice bunch of people.” In 2010 he visited classmate Larry Morse in hospice.

Sources: Julius Richman obituary, Providence Journal, September 28, 1996, p. A-08; wedding notice, Providence Sunday Journal, June 1, 1986, p. K-10; Jeannette Richman obituary, Jewish Rhode Island, August 20, 2025; class reunion records.
