Rebecca Elaine (Barnes) Straw

Rebecca Elaine Barnes — known as Becky Straw — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Allen Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was on the Dean’s Council, National Honor Society, Social Committee (co-chair), Class Council, Ways and Means Committee, Wrestling Club, and Leader Corps.

Public records identify her as Rebecca Elaine Straw of Wrentham, Massachusetts, married to Jeff (Jeffrey Tucker) Straw. Their children are Ryan and Elaina.
