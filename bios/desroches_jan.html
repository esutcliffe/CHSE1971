Jan DesRoches St. Pierre

Jan DesRoches was a member of the Cranston High School East Class of 1971, though she does not appear in the senior yearbook that year. Her married name is St. Pierre. She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Jan, please let us know.
