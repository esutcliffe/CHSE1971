Carol Safford DeBeradinis

Carol Evelyn Safford — known as Carol Safford DeBeradinis — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Grace Street in Cranston. In high school she was Mixed Chorus, German Club, Folk Workshop, and Orchestra.

On the class site an undated note celebrates her first granddaughter, Kayla. In April 2014 class notes she celebrated another granddaughter, Lily Paige, born February 18, 2014.
