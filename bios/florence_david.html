David Joseph Florence

David Florence grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East he played basketball and belonged to the Basketball Club and the Thyrsus Club as a sophomore. He served on the Junior Class Council, then on the Senior Class Council and the Student Council as a senior. His parents, John and M. Louise (Veronneau) Florence, lived in Cranston for 30 years.

David went into business in Rhode Island's metal-finishing industry: in the 1980s he was treasurer and later president of API, an electroplating company, and when it was sold to Sprague Metal Box Co. in 1987 he became Sprague's vice president of finance and operations. He made his home in East Greenwich, and in 1989 he served as first vice president of the Rhode Island chapter of the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation. He has stayed in touch with the class and attended the 32nd reunion in 2003, where he was photographed with classmates Dianne Centofante Miller and Dan Barry.
