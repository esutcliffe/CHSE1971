Robert James Thompson

Robert Thompson grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East he was in the A.V.A. for three years. As a junior he belonged to the Electronics Club and was vice president of the Engineering and Drafting Club, and as a sophomore he was on the swimming and cross country teams.

Robert stayed connected with the class after graduation. He contributed the group photo from our 10th reunion, held in November 1981 at the Quonset Officers' Club in North Kingstown. We would be glad to hear from Robert to fill in the years since.
