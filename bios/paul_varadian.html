Paul Norad Varadian

Paul Norad Varadian graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Willow Drive in Cranston. In high school he was Thunderbolt Staff (sports editor junior and senior years), Cross Country, Wrestling, Indoor Track, Outdoor Track, U.R.I. Computer Workshop, R.I. Boys State Wrestling Club, Track Club, and Ski Club.

Public memory and class notes place him at the center of Armenia’s early Winter Olympic story — organizing Armenia’s 1994 Lillehammer bobsled entry and long service as Armenia Olympic Committee international representative / Chef de Mission — after a U.S. bobsled background (class notes also used “skeleton” wording; soft both). Soft, dated: August 2010 class notes carried a stem-cell donor plea when his wife contracted leukemia — date-stamp; do not invent her name or outcome. Father Haig Varadian’s name went on Cranston East’s new science wing (dedicated 17 June 2009). Soft 2014 news placed him in the Newton, Massachusetts area.
