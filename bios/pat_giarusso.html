Patricia Lynn Giarrusso

Patricia Lynn Giarrusso — known as Pat Giarrusso — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up in Cranston. In high school she was Wrestling Manager and Wrestling Club president, National Honor Society, Color Guard, and Ways and Means Committee.

She has long been the class’s reunion treasurer. Classmate Pat Dewey married Pat's brother, Paul, so the Class of 1971's two Pat Giarrussos are sisters-in-law. Classmates offered condolences after her mother’s death in February 2013. She has also been associated with Peppermint Patti’s, a children’s products business in Wakefield, and with teaching in the South Kingstown schools in the mid-2000s.
