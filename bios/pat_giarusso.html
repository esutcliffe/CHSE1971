Patricia Lynn Giarusso

Patricia Lynn Giarusso — known as Pat Giarusso — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Magnolia Street in Cranston. In high school she was Wrestling Manager and Wrestling Club president, National Honor Society, Color Guard, and Ways and Means Committee.

She has long been the class’s reunion treasurer — distinct from classmate Pat Dewey Giarrusso. Classmates offered condolences after her mother’s death in February 2013. She has also been associated with Peppermint Patti’s, a children’s products business in Wakefield, and with teaching in the South Kingstown schools in the mid-2000s.
