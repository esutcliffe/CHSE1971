Beverly Lee (Samson) Goyer

Beverly Samson grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she was a member of Future Nurses as a senior. The class lists give her married name as Goyer.

Her probable parents, though we have not confirmed this, are Joseph S. and Sylvia S. (Preblud) Samson, who lived in Cranston for about 30 years. Their Providence Journal obituaries from 1985 and 1989 each name a daughter, Beverly Gayer of Warwick. The obituaries do not give Beverly’s middle name, so the link is our best inference. The newspaper spells the married name "Gayer," while the class lists spell it "Goyer," and we have not settled which is right.

She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Beverly, please let us know.
