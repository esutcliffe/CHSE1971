Anna Maria (Dias) Laughren

Anna Maria Dias — later Anna (Dias) Laughren / Anna Laughren — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Auburn Street in Cranston. In high school she was Stockroom Assistant, Future Nurses Club (first vice-president junior year; secretary senior year), Hockey Club, Ecology Action Movement, and Office Practice.

By 2011 class notes she had lived in Rockville, Maryland since 1984 as a nurse manager at a small Montgomery County hospital, with husband Tom in FDA leadership and daughters Kelly (visual arts, UMBC) and Emily (computer science at Boston University near Fenway) in college then — still calling Rhode Island home (Sand Hill Cove; reconnect with Kathy Stamp). A long public career trail continues through MedStar Montgomery Medical Center (performance improvement / risk / regulatory compliance) and later Joint Commission field surveyor work, with nursing degrees from Rhode Island College and Boston University.
