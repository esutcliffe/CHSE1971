Peter R. Dailey

Peter R. Dailey graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Essex Street in Cranston with his brother Michael. In high school he wrestled (and was in Wrestling Club) sophomore through senior year, and played soccer (and Soccer Club) as a senior.

Public records place him over the years in East Greenwich and Warwick, Rhode Island; in Hermosa Beach, Playa del Rey, and Three Rivers, California; and in the Deposit, New York area, where directories list him in the same household as his brother Michael. He is not believed to be the Peter Robert Dailey of Tampa who died in 2001.
