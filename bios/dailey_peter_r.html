Peter R. Dailey

Peter R. Dailey graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He was born on April 13, 1953, in Warwick, Rhode Island, the twin brother of classmate Michael F. “Mike” Dailey, and a son of Lester and Nancy Dailey. He grew up in Cranston with Mike. In high school he wrestled (and was in Wrestling Club) sophomore through senior year, and played soccer (and Soccer Club) as a senior.

Public records place him over the years in East Greenwich and Warwick, Rhode Island; in Hermosa Beach, Playa del Rey, and Three Rivers, California; and in the Deposit, New York area, where directories list him in the same household as his brother Michael.

Mike died on October 1, 2026. His obituary says that in his final years Mike “was blessed with the unwavering support and care of his twin brother, Pete,” and the family thanked Pete “for his boundless love, care, and devotion to Michael, particularly in his later years.” Mike’s obituary is on his In Memoriam page.

He is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Peter, please let us know.
