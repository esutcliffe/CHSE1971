Jessica Gerarda Morris

Jessica Gerarda Morris graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Caporal Street in Cranston. In high school she was Student Council, Dean’s Council (secretary, then president), Cheerleading, National Honor Society, Tutoring, Ways and Means, Italian Club, Activities Committee, Football Follies, and Class Council alternate. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Outstanding Student and Most Likely to Succeed (both with Jacob Adler).
