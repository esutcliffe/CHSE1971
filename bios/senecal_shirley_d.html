Shirley D. (Senecal) Hendrix

Shirley D. Senecal graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. The yearbook lists her home on South Clarendon Street in Cranston. In high school she was on Thunderbolt as a senior.

As an adult she has used the name Shirley Senecal Hendrix. Public profiles place her in Sandown, New Hampshire, after earlier career work in Georgia. She lists Rhode Island College in her background. Her documented work has been in talent acquisition and small business: Talent Hunters in the Cumming, Georgia area, later Southern New Hampshire roofing talent-acquisition / ownership signals with Mighty Dog Roofing, and more recently AIEIEIO LLC, a New Hampshire firm she describes as AI consulting for small businesses (the name is a playful twist on “AI” plus E-I-E-I-O). Public posts place that consulting work from April 2026.

Classmates are still hoping to reconnect. She is not listed among deceased classmates. A 2011 class missing-list tip used the surname Klug; that tip has not been independently confirmed, and Hendrix is the stronger documentary married name.
