Linda Ann (DiMuccio) Pontarelli

Linda DiMuccio grew up in Cranston, a daughter of Ralph J. DiMuccio Sr., who owned the former Centerdale Floor Covering Co. in North Providence and worked at Lincoln Greyhound Park. At Cranston East she took Office Practice as a senior. She later married and took the name Pontarelli, and in 1998 she was living in North Providence.

Linda gave her time to the class’s 32nd reunion in 2003, helping out with last-minute details, and she appears in the reunion photo gallery as Linda DiMuccio Pontarelli.
