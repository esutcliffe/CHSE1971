Patricia Dewey Giarrusso

Patricia J. Dewey — known as Patricia Dewey Giarrusso or Pat Dewey Giarrusso — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Lyndon Road in Cranston. In high school she was in Thyrsus Club, Office Practice, Cafeteria Marshal, Library Aide, and Hockey Club.

She has helped with classmate phone outreach and party check-in, and she appears in 25th–50th reunion materials under that name — distinct from classmate Pat Giarusso, the class treasurer. Classmates offered condolences after her mother’s death in May 2012. She has worked as an administrative assistant at South Kingstown High School in Wakefield.
