Ellen Lee (Galkin) Kenner

Ellen Galkin grew up in Cranston, the oldest of the three daughters of Robert T. “Bob” Galkin and Wini (Blacher) Galkin. At Cranston East she sang in the A Cappella Choir for three years and was its secretary as a senior. She was a cheerleader, a member of the National Honor Society, secretary of the French Club, and took part in the Junior Alliance Française, the Leader Corps, the Social Committee, the Football Follies, Toastmasters Youth Leadership and the Spanish Club.

She graduated from Brown University in 1975 with a degree in biology, following her father (Brown ’49). Later, encouraged by her uncle Warren Galkin, she earned an M.A. (1989) and a Ph.D. (1992) in clinical psychology at the University of Rhode Island, then was a clinical fellow at Harvard Medical School. After six years at the Blackstone Valley Psychological Institute, she opened her own practice in Rhode Island in 1998.

Since 1997 Dr. Ellen Kenner has hosted the nationally syndicated radio program “The Rational Basis of Happiness,” which continues as a podcast. She co-wrote The Selfish Path to Romance: How to Love with Passion & Reason (2011) with Dr. Edwin A. Locke and has taught at URI and Rhode Island College. She and her husband, Harris Kenner, have two grown children. The couple came to the class’s 32nd reunion in 2003.
