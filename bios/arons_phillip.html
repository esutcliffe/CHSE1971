Phillip Marc Arons, Class of 1971

Phillip Marc Arons grew up in Cranston, the son of Max and Irma (Wallack) Arons, with a sister, Vicki.

At Cranston High School East, Phillip was drawn to the work of getting the word out. As a junior he helped with Junior Class Publicity and was H.R. Alternate for Thunderbolt. As a senior he joined the Thunderbolt staff, sold advertising for the Cranstonian, and belonged to Civic Leadership and the Junior Alliance Français.

By the late 1980s Phillip had settled in Westboro, Massachusetts, where he was living when his father died in 1987 and his mother in 1990.

He may well be the Phillip M. Arons who built a career of more than forty years in the specialty food business. That Phillip earned a B.S. in chemistry, worked briefly as a chemist in the late 1970s, and went on to become national director of category management at UNFI, a distributor of natural, organic, and specialty foods. He was a judge for the Specialty Food Association’s sofi awards, and his professional listing places him in Mendon, Massachusetts, not far from Westboro. We haven’t been able to confirm that he is our classmate.

Phillip is one of the classmates we are still hoping to find. If you know where he is, or if you are Phillip, we would love to hear from you.
