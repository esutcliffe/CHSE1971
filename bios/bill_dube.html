William P. Dubé

William P. Dubé — known as Bill Dubé — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Grace Street in Cranston. In high school he was in Ecology Action Movement, Photography Club, Electronics Club, Thyrsus Club, Folk Club, Poetry Seminars, and the Junior Talent Show.

After the Air Force, mechanic and electrician work in Rhode Island, and the blizzard of 1978, he drove west to Colorado, earned a mechanical-engineering degree from the University of Colorado, and built a research-engineering career — first at the National Bureau of Standards, then at NOAA / CIRES in Boulder, including air-quality field work in places such as Utah and Hong Kong. On evenings and weekends he built the KillaCycle, the electric drag motorcycle that made him a public figure in electric racing (quarter-mile times moved over the years; the bike was retired in 2014).

Around the 30th-reunion era he wrote that he had just married for the second time and had four stepchildren. Later, in December 2007, he met Eva Håkansson; they married in 2009 in an EV-themed wedding. Together they continued electric land-speed projects (including KillaJoule and Green Envy), moved to New Zealand, and founded KiwiFil. He came the furthest to the Class of 1971 50th reunion in June 2022 — from New Zealand. As of 2025–2026 their public racing pages still describe him as Eva’s husband and co-builder, with testing at Loring, Maine, and a Bonneville plan.
