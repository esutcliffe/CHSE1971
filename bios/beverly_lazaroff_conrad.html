Beverly Lazaroff Conrad

Beverly Ann Lazaroff — known as Beverly Lazaroff Conrad — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Harwood Street in Cranston. In high school she was Future Teachers of America, Library Aids, Thunderbolt circulation manager, Cranstonian homeroom representative, and Mixed Chorus.

A December 2005 class-site note says she graduated from the University of Tennessee in Knoxville as a speech pathologist, was working in adult speech pathology, had moved around the country, and was living near Seattle with her husband and two children (then a high-school junior and a college sophomore), while staying in touch with several classmates. In December 2012 she reconnected from the greater Seattle area, explained that distance had kept her from earlier reunions, hoped to make a June gathering, and asked to be put back on the class email list after hearing from Barbara Shapiro (Buckler). Do not confuse with classmate Beverly Ann Brisson.
