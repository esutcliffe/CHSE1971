Sandra Gail “Sandy” Agronick, Class of 1971

Sandra Gail “Sandy” Agronick grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Perry S. Agronick (1926–1984), who owned Sessions Electronics, and Frances (Kushner) Agronick (1929–2020), an administrative assistant at IBM.

In January 1966 Sandy became a bas mitzvah at the Cranston Jewish Center, one of three girls who marked the day together at the same service. At Cranston East she played with the Hockey Club as a junior and senior, joined the Thyrsus Club as a junior, and danced with Modern Dance as a senior, when she also served as an alternate homeroom representative.

By the spring of 1984 Sandy was living in Newport.

We believe that a few years later she went about as far from Narragansett Bay as a Rhode Islander can go. A Sandy Agronick turns up in Anchorage, Alaska, at the start of the 1990s. In June 1990 she finished a long race there in 1:39:26, a time that suggests a half marathon, and in June 1992 “Sandy G. Agronick” finished in 1:45:13. In January 1991 the Anchorage Daily News photographed her relaxing amid the greenery of the downtown Federal Building atrium for a story about how atriums brighten the long winter for office workers. That spring the Anchorage Center for Families, which helped families under stress, thanked her among its volunteers for the 1990–91 year. None of these items mentions Cranston, so we can’t be certain this is our classmate, but the name is an uncommon one and the dates fit.

Sandy was back in Rhode Island by our 35th reunion at Yawgoo in 2006, and by 2020 she was living in Narragansett. She is still Sandy Agronick to all of us, and she joined the class again in August 2026 for our 55th reunion at Chelo’s.
