Jean Ann Colaneri McGovern

Jean Ann Colaneri — later Jean Colaneri-McGovern / Jean Colaneri McGovern — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Laurel Hill Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was Thunderbolt Representative, Football Follies, and Data Processing.

After a long stretch at the Cranston YMCA she retired around 2008, took up yoga, and taught yoga at another Y — advice classmates heard from her era: be flexible, breathe, and let go. She was listed among class teachers. Soft bereavement: husband and classmate Richard McGovern died 4 December 2013; condolences went to Jean, their children, and grandchildren. She appears by full married name with Lauren Goding Johnson on 50th-reunion photos. Soft later trail: yoga / Dragonfly Studios work in the Coventry area. Exact birth date not found.
