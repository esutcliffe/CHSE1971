Arnold Kirshenbaum, MD, is dual certified in Pediatrics and Allergy and Immunology. An accomplished clinical investigator, he has been involved in numerous research projects that contribute to patient care for future generations. Patients appreciate his compassionate and thorough care for people of all ages.

Education: Doctor of Medicine, Georgetown University (Washington, D.C.); Pediatric residency, Georgetown University and the University of California, San Francisco; Allergy and Immunology fellowship, National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases. He is board certified in Pediatrics (American Board of Pediatrics) and in Allergy and Immunology (ABAI).

He practices with AllerVie Health in Glenn Dale, Maryland (12150 Annapolis Rd, Suite 108). Beyond medicine, he is an avid composer and producer of music and enjoys riding his scooter.
