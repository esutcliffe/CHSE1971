Debra Jean Donnelly Cronin

Debra Jean Donnelly — later Debra Donnelly Cronin — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Waterman Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was Thyrsus Club (treasurer senior year), Italian Club, Leader Corps, Ski Club, Modern Dance, Class Council, Thunderbolt Homeroom Representative, Charisma, and P.A. System.

She began teaching later in life — class notes say she started around age 51 — and taught eighth-grade algebra. Soft, dated medical: January 2012 notes recount a hard eye-health chapter (retinal melanoma treated with a radioactive plaque, then retinal detachment and emergency surgery in the same eye) during medical leave from teaching. She was later listed among class teachers. Exact birth date not found (do not reverse-engineer from the “age 51” note).

Living / in touch as of last class data.
