David Charles Lanni

David Charles Lanni — known as Dave — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Florida Avenue in Cranston.

Class notes in the early 2010s followed his Michigan HR career — ADP TotalSource, Sears Holdings talent work, then VP of Human Resources at Arden Companies — including meeting Michelle Obama and Jill Biden at a 2011 Joining Forces military employment event, a 2012 triple graduation year for sons David Jr., Derek, and Daniel, a Cancun graduation trip through Hurricane Ernesto, and Red Sox Nation spotting at a Sox/Tigers playoff in Detroit. He and his wife Josette have lived in the Michigan area.

Soft family note: brother John E. Lanni Jr., longtime Cranston councilman and Council President, died October 14, 2020.
