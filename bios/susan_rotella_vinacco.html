Susan Mary Rotella Vinacco

Susan Mary Rotella — later Susan Rotella Vinacco — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Woodbine Street in Cranston. In high school she was Cheerleading, Retailing, Thunderbolt Homeroom Representative, and DECA.

She appears at the 32nd reunion as Susan Rotella Vinacco with Cosima Fallon, Florence Cardogno Vocolla, and Sherry Halverson Cirelli. Class notes thank her for calling missing classmates before the 2013 birthday party. Soft bereavement: mother Marjorie E. (Brothers) Rotella died 30 September 2013; that notice lists survivor daughter Susan Rotella of Cranston (Ed’s November 2013 “mother died previous month” aligns).
