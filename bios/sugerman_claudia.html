Claudia Beth (Sugerman) Akerstein

Claudia Sugerman grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Nathaniel "Nat" and Beverly (Gomberg) Sugerman. Her father started the Beatrix Jewelry Company and in 1951 founded the Providence Metallizing Company, a finisher for Rhode Island's costume-jewelry trade, which he ran for 40 years. At Cranston East Claudia took Modern Dance as a junior, and as a senior she was on the Debating Team and belonged to the Junior Alliance Francaise, the Foster Parents Committee and the Thyrsus Club.

Claudia married and became Claudia Akerstein. She lived in Cranston through at least 1991, and in the 1980s she was active in the Providence area. In 1986 she was one of the cooks for a benefit dinner for the Providence Hebrew Day School and served on the arrangements committee for an art auction and dinner to aid local schools. By 2018 she was living in Massachusetts. She is one of the classmates the reunion committee has been trying to find. If you know how to reach Claudia, please let us know.
