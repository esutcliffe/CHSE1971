Corrine Ellen Kilpeck Bernal / Fortune

Corrine Ellen Kilpeck — later Corrine (Kilpeck) Bernal, still later Corrine Kilpeck Fortune — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Garden Street in Cranston. In high school she was Italian Club, Football Follies, Thyrsus Club, Cranston Follies, Cranstonian Advertising Staff, Thunderbolt Staff, and Tutoring.

Classmates thanked (Kilpeck) Bernal at the 31st reunion and photographed her (caption spelling Corinne) with Kathleen Mooney Gouveria and Helen Desmond at the 32nd. April 2009 class notes celebrate daughter Marissa in the Cranston East/West Disney-sponsored *High School Musical* coverage.
