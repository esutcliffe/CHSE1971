Donna Marie (Lepizzera) Caldamone

Donna Lepizzera grew up in Cranston, a daughter of Fiore and Emily (Seamans) Lepizzera. Her father was an electrician for more than 50 years. He was head of the electrical shop at Brown University, president of the Brown University Credit Union, and a trustee of the St. Mary’s Feast Society. Her mother, born in Providence, belonged to the Knightsville Library Association and the Women’s Auxiliary of the St. Mary’s Feast Society. Both of her parents died in 2014.

At Cranston East Donna played in the Hockey Club as a junior and served on the Social Committee as a junior and senior. She sang in the Mixed Chorus as a junior and senior and took Office Practice as a senior.

Donna Lepizzera Caldamone makes her home in Cranston. She served on the planning committee for the class’s 40th reunion in 2011, and she attended the 50th reunion.
