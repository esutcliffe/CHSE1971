Todd C. Moody, Class of 1971

Todd Moody, the son of Mr. and Mrs. William V. Moody, who were living in Burrillville by 1978, went on from Cranston High School East to spend more than forty years thinking, writing and teaching about some of the biggest questions there are.

Todd went on to Rhode Island College, where he earned his B.A. in English in 1977, and the following spring, on April 22, 1978, he married Constance Geary of Providence at St. Sebastian’s Church, with the reception at Metacomet Country Club in East Providence. Among the ushers was our own classmate Brian McMahon.

Todd then turned from English to philosophy, earning his Ph.D. at Temple University in 1983. That same year he joined the philosophy faculty at Saint Joseph’s University in Philadelphia, where he has taught ever since, twice winning the university’s Merit Award for Teaching, in 1988 and 2001. Today he is Professor Emeritus of Philosophy and still teaches part-time.

Along the way he wrote books that bring hard questions within reach of ordinary readers, including Philosophy and Artificial Intelligence, Does God Exist? and, in 2023, Life and Death: Philosophical Reflections. His 1994 article “Conversations with Zombies,” about whether a being could act exactly like us without any inner experience, is still widely cited by people who study the mind.

Todd and Connie have made their home in Philadelphia since 1986. They have kept their ties to the class too. Todd turned up at our 32nd reunion in 2003, and the two of them were on the list for the 40th in 2011.
