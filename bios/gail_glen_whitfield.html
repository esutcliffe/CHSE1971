Gail Glen Whitfield

Gail M. Glen — known in class circles as Gail Glen Whitfield — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Preston Drive in Cranston. In high school she was A Cappella Choir (president senior year; assistant director junior and senior years), Leader Corps (lieutenant commander senior year), Football Follies, Spanish Club, and Class Council alternate. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Most Popular (with Daniel Barry).

On the class site she appears in many 32nd-reunion photos under the Whitfield name, including with Jeff Whitfield. Soft, dated: a January 2014 class remembrance recalled math teacher and coach Mr. Alvin Clegg.
