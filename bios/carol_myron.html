Carol Ann Myron

Carol Ann Myron graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Speck Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was A Cappella Choir, Dean’s Council (treasurer junior year; vice president senior year), Folk Workshop, and Football Follies. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Most Musical (with Ronald Stabile).

Soft, dated class notes (2009–2012) recall her son Corbin’s Full Sail Music Business graduation and move toward Nashville, and her own note that she was still singing; she remembered influential teachers including Ruth Roseman and Paul Mancini. She appears in labeled 32nd-reunion photos and is captioned at the 50th. Soft: no dedicated class bio page; later married-name forms unverified this pass. Exact birth date not found.
