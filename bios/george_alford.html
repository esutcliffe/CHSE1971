George Alford

George Alford has been treated in class channels as a Cranston High School East Class of 1971 classmate — named on the 2002 31st mini-reunion list and in Ed DeJesus’s 2010–2012 updates — though he had no yearbook card in the slim file until this add. Classmates following those updates knew him for small roles with his wife Linda in the local film Gotta Find Barry, later local-film credits including Polypore and the 2013 RISD short Donner Party: The Musical (as George Donner), a formative memory of Metal Shop teacher Mr. Bishop, and — after military and mechanical work — a 2012 retirement-era plan for an auto-detailing business he called “The Wizard of Carz.” Soft Ed-era notes: February 2011 rollover (on the mend); one 2012 note recalls Navy, another Air Force retirement — do not collapse into a single service story. Exact birth date not found.

Living / in touch as of last class data. No yearbook crop.
