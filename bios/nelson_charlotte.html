Charlotte Mary (Nelson) Sponsler

Charlotte Nelson grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she was in Modern Dance, Class Council, the Thunderbolt Homeroom Representatives, the Hockey Club and the Ski Club. Right after graduation she earned an associate’s degree in nursing at Rhode Island Community College (1971–73), then a B.S.N. and an M.S.N. at West Virginia University, becoming a family nurse practitioner in 2001. Since 2004 she has practiced in Wasilla, Alaska, at the Alaska Brain Center. She is married to Dr. Jeffrey Sponsler and uses the married name Sponsler; professionally she is often listed as Charlotte Nelson. She was at the class’s 32nd reunion in 2003.
