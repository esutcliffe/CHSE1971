Susan Lee (Sheehan) Simm, Class of 1971

Susan Lee Sheehan grew up in Cranston, the daughter of William A. Sheehan Jr. and Elinor H. (Ford) Sheehan.

At Cranston High School East, Susan marched with the Majorettes as a junior and senior and led them as head majorette her senior year. She belonged to the Future Teachers all three years and was the club’s vice president as a senior. She also played with the Hockey Club as a junior and senior, joined the French Club as a sophomore, served as homeroom representative for the Thunderbolt as a sophomore, sat on the Class Council as a junior, where she was also an alternate, and took part in Football Follies as a junior and senior.

Her years with the Future Teachers seem to have pointed the way. In June 1975 a Susan L. Sheehan was listed among the education graduates in the Providence Journal’s commencement coverage, probably at Rhode Island College, though the clipping doesn’t name the school.

Susan married Stanton W. Simm Jr. and made her home with him in Stonington, Connecticut, where town records show the two of them owning a house since the early 1980s. When her mother died in 1995 and her father in 2001, their obituaries named her as Susan Simm of Connecticut, and by 2001 of Stonington.

Susan is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach her, please let us know.
