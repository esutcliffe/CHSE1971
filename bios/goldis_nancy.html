Nancy Lynne (Goldis) Iacono

Nancy Goldis grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Irving and May (Bornstein) Goldis. Her father was a salesman for Electrolux for 20 years and then for Highlights children's magazine for 15 years, retiring in 1991. Her mother was a lifelong Rhode Islander who later lived in Warwick for 25 years and then in North Kingstown.

As an adult Nancy built a creative career in custom sewing and costume design under her business, TomCat Designs. Around 2009 her work included vinyl animal models for National Geographic's Crittercam, parade costumes for a Terracotta Warriors event, work for Disney on Ice, and designs for retail lines sold at Target and Wal-Mart. She and classmate Tom Iacono are married and live in Narragansett, where they ran the Best Kept Secret Bed & Breakfast and offered classmates special rates at reunion time. She has traveled widely, including trips to Israel, Jordan and Egypt, Costa Rica and Turkey, where she took a hot-air balloon ride over Cappadocia.

Nancy has been a regular at class gatherings. She appears in photos from the 32nd reunion and at the 35th, where she was pictured checking shirts with Beverly Brisson DeFelice.
