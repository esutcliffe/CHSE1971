Paula Tagliaferri Moretti, Class of 1971

Paula Ann Tagliaferri grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Paul Tagliaferri and Norma G. Casacalenda. Her mother was a personnel manager and store purchasing agent at the Woolworth's in Garden City Center, and the family belonged to St. Mary's Parish.

At Cranston High School East, Paula was a member of DECA as a senior and served on the Class Council as a sophomore. She graduated in 1971.

Paula married Everett R. "Corky" Moretti Jr., and they raised three children in Cranston, where she has lived all her life. Their youngest, Brian E. Moretti, graduated from Cranston West in 2005, attended Johnson & Wales and Canisius, and became assistant sports information director at St. Bonaventure. Brian died in March 2014 at just 26. The Rhode Island Senate passed a resolution in his memory that year, and a scholarship fund in his name is held at the Rhode Island Foundation. Paula's mother died later in 2014.

She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Paula, please let us know.
