Timothy J. Harrington

Timothy J. Harrington graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Wheeler Avenue in the Edgewood section of Cranston. In high school he was Football (co-captain senior year), Hockey, Baseball, Football Club, and Student Council senior-class delegate. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives he was named Most Athletic (with Carmen Troiano).

Public sports memory places him as the older Harrington brother who went to Dartmouth and played Big Green hockey in the early 1970s; public profiles index a Dartmouth B.A. in Economics in those years. Soft, dated: his mother’s October 2020 obituary places Timothy and Pam in Richmond, Virginia.
