William David Ernst

William Ernst grew up in Cranston, the son of George E. “Evey” Ernst and Violet (Elmer) Ernst. His father was a project engineer with New England Telephone for 44 years and was the driving force behind Rhode Island’s North-South Trail.

At Cranston East William was a distance runner. He ran cross country as a junior and senior and ran indoor and outdoor track all three years, and he belonged to the Track Club as a junior and senior and the Ski Club as a sophomore. As a senior he was an alternate for the U.R.I. Oceanography Seminar.

William was living in Tacoma, Washington, as of 2002. He is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach William, please let us know.
