Roberta Maria Martellucci

Roberta Maria Martellucci graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Pendleton Street in Cranston. In high school she was Class Council and H.R. Delegate, Ski Club, Charisma, Football Follies, Talent Show, Folk Show, G.A.A., and Folk Club.

Class notes from the 2009–2013 era sketch decades of church and missionary music — guitar, singing, and teaching in orphanages for more than twenty-five years; a Puerto Rico church/school build trip (building, guitar, singing, face-painting at the University of Mayagüez); and a Baltimore church network. She forwarded Baltimore–DC blizzard photos around 2010 and stayed in Facebook touch with classmate Dean Bengtson. Soft later trail: Abingdon, Maryland / Bambini Family Entertainment (children’s entertainers) may be the same person. Exact birth date not found.
