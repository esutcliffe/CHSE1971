Eugene Henry Blair

Eugene Henry Blair — known as Gene — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Taft Street in Cranston. In high school he was Band officer and Head Drum Major, Indoor Track, and Soccer.

Classmates following Ed’s updates knew him as a reliable finder of missing classmates’ contacts, often alongside Twin Oaks life in Cranston with wife Joanne. An April 2014 note celebrated married sons Nicholas (Channel 12 after WPRO) and Joseph (Mount Pleasant High PE/coach) and an Italy-to-Spain trip. Public news in April 2021 marked Gene’s 50 years at Twin Oaks, where he began in September 1970. Soft family note: father died October 2011.
