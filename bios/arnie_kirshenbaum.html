Arnold S. Kirshenbaum

Arnold S. Kirshenbaum — known as Arnie — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Hemlin Road in Cranston. In high school he was National Honor Society, Band and Stage Band, Indoor Track, Charisma poetry staff, URI physics and computer seminars, Science Club, and Audio Visual Aids.

He did his undergraduate studies at Providence College, where he roomed with classmate Ernie Sutcliffe, then earned an M.D. from Georgetown, trained in pediatrics at Georgetown and UCSF, served in the Navy after a medical-school scholarship, and completed an allergy/immunology fellowship at NIAID. He is dual board-certified in Pediatrics and Allergy & Immunology, long associated with mast-cell research in NIAID’s Laboratory of Allergic Diseases, and practices at AllerVie Health in Glenn Dale, Maryland. Outside the clinic he composes and produces music — including work noted in a 2020 profile on his Millennial Symphony — and still rides a scooter for fun.

He and his wife Vivian have five grown children. Do not confuse with classmate Merrill Kirshenbaum.
