Lynne M. Freedman Turkus

Lynne M. Freedman — later Lynne Freedman Turkus — grew up in Cranston and graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. In high school she served on the Class Council (alternate as a junior and delegate as a senior) and belonged to the Thunderbolt staff and the Art Service Council.

Lynne has been a familiar helper for our class reunions. She was photographed at the 32nd reunion with Dan Barry, Larry Morse, Eric Gauvin, Domenic Dantuono, and Brenda Pandozzi D’Aguanno, and was thanked among the helpers for our 40th reunion.

On AlumniClass.com, Lynne notes that she is widowed, retired, and living in Florida.
