Lynne M. Freedman Turkus

Lynne M. Freedman — later Lynne Freedman Turkus — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Tome Street in Cranston. In high school she was Class Council (alternate junior year; delegate senior year), Thunderbolt, and Art Service Council.

She is a familiar reunion helper — photographed at the 32nd with Dan Barry, Larry Morse, Eric Gauvin, Domenic Dantuono, and classmates including Brenda Pandozzi D’Aguanno, and thanked again among 40th helpers.
