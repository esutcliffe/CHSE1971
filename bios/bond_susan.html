Susan Bond, Class of 1971

Susan Waltina Bond was born in Providence in September 1953, the daughter of Walter Bond Jr., who was born in Bolton, England, and Ruth E. Ericsson. Her middle name appears to echo her father's. Her mother grew up in the Lordship section of Stratford, Connecticut, and worked as a clerical supervisor at Chance-Vought in Stratford, later at Shepard's, and in Fleet Bank's Mastercard department.

At Cranston High School East, Susan was in the Ski Club and Track Club, served on the Social Committee in her junior and senior years, and on the Ways and Means Committee as a junior. She graduated in 1971.

Susan went on to work as a title examiner. She has lived in Cranston and later in Glocester, RI. Her mother died in May 1997 and is buried at the Rhode Island Veterans Cemetery in Exeter.
