Reba Lee Weintraub Novich

Reba Weintraub grew up in Cranston and attended the Providence Hebrew Day School before Cranston East. At CHSE she belonged to the Future Teachers as a sophomore, junior and senior, and was the club’s president in her senior year. She was a member of the National Honor Society as a junior and senior, and of the French Club and the Thyrsus Club as a junior. As a senior she served on the Ways and Means Committee and the Cranstonian advertising staff.

Reba married Barry Novich of Providence, and they have made their home in Gaithersburg, Maryland.

A public LinkedIn profile for a Reba Novich in the Washington, D.C. area lists a B.A. in psychology from the University of Connecticut and an M.S.W. from the University of Maryland, Baltimore, followed by a long career in public health and aging policy in the D.C. area. That work includes the National Association of State Units on Aging, the National Osteoporosis Foundation / NIH ORBD-National Resource Center and the Association of Community Cancer Centers, and she is principal consultant of Novich and Associates. We have not confirmed that this career information is Reba’s, because the profile does not name her high school.

Reba has stayed in touch with the class. On September 9, 2006, she came to the 35th reunion dinner at Yawgoo Bakes.
