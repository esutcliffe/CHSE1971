Bruce Marc Weiner, Class of 1971

Bruce Marc Weiner grew up in Cranston, the son of Edward Weiner (1926–1985), a furniture consultant and World War II Navy veteran, and Estelle (Mandell) Weiner (1926–2010), a teacher's aide in Cranston for 35 years.

At Cranston High School East, Bruce played baseball as a sophomore, ran indoor track as a junior and outdoor track as a junior and senior, and belonged to the Track Club as a junior. As a senior he played soccer and joined the Soccer Club, and he served on the Class Council and as a homeroom representative.

Bruce's brother, Scott, and his sister, Joyce, who graduated from Cranston East in 1973, grew up in the same Cranston household. Bruce was still living in Cranston when his father died in 1985 and when his mother died in 2010.

In May 1976 a Bruce M. Weiner was listed among college graduates in the Providence Journal's commencement coverage, possibly at the University of Rhode Island, though the clipping we have doesn't show the school or degree.

A Bruce Weiner of Cranston was married for some 50 years to Stephanie (Campagnone) Weiner, who died in 2026; we have not confirmed that this is our classmate.

Bruce is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach him, please let us know.
