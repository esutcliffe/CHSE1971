Lauren Lee Goding Johnson

Lauren Lee Goding — later Lauren (Goding) Johnson / Lauren Goding Johnson — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Garden Street in Cranston. In high school she was Cheerleader, National Honor Society, Wrestling Club, and Mixed Chorus.

Class notes remember her helping find missing classmates and list her among class teachers. Soft bereavement: her father died in June (reported July 2012) — do not invent his name. She appears on 31st/32nd reunion helper and caller lists and front-and-center with Jean Colaneri McGovern on 50th-reunion photos (caption typo “Johsnon” still means Lauren Goding Johnson); also captioned at the 50th with Barbara Haggis Thompson and Maryann Pezza Matano.
