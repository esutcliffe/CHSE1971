Lauren Lee Goding Johnson

Lauren Lee Goding — later Lauren (Goding) Johnson / Lauren Goding Johnson — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up in Cranston. In high school she was Cheerleader, National Honor Society, Wrestling Club, and Mixed Chorus.

Class notes remember her helping find missing classmates and list her among class teachers. Her father died in June 2012. She appears on 31st/32nd reunion helper and caller lists and front-and-center with Jean Colaneri McGovern on 50th-reunion photos; also captioned at the 50th with Barbara Haggis Thompson and Maryann Pezza Matano.

Her Mother’s Class Ring

Lauren’s mother, Gertrude E. “Gig” (Slade) Goding, who died in 1975, graduated from Cranston High School in 1946, and her gold class ring is set with a green stone and carries a crest of three cranes on a shield with a ribbon below. It is the emblem of the City of Cranston, taken from the family arms of Gov. Samuel Cranston, whose motto Dum Vigilo Curo means “While I watch, I care.” Francis A. Cranston presented the arms to the city, and the same seal appears on the covers of the Cranstonian, the Cranston High yearbook, in the 1920s.
