Ernest Leonard Rheaume

Ernest Leonard Rheaume — known as Ernie — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Bolton Street in Cranston. In high school he was in the National Honor Society, and participated in the U.R.I. Chemistry Seminars, and the R.I.C. History Colloquia.

He is married to Jeanne (DiPrete) Rheaume, Cranston West Class of 1973. Their three children are Ernest (“Ernie”), who is married to Kim Gilmore and is living in Colorado; Nicole (“Nikki”), who is married to Ben Scheff and is living in Vermont with their two children; and Christina (“Chrissy”), who is married to Juan Mendez Martinez and is living in Spain with their two children.

He holds an M.S. in Finance and an MBA from the University of Rhode Island. He has worked as a Database Administrator for Old Stone Bank, as a Director of Software Services for Hasbro and as an Epic Cache System Manager for Rhode Island Hospital.

He has been involved with the Boy Scouts of America as an adult volunteer since 1990, receiving the Silver Beaver Award from the Narragansett Council in 1999. He has served as the Cubmaster for Pack 40 in Cranston and as an Assistant Scoutmaster for Troop 66 in Garden City. He has also served as the Master of Ceremonies at local Eagle Scout Courts of Honor. Ernie earned the rank of Eagle Scout in 1967 with Troop 309 in Biddeford, ME. His son Ernie earned the rank of Eagle Scout in 1999 with Troop 66, Garden City, RI.

Ernie has been a steady Class Reunion volunteer, assuming the Reunion Committee Classmate communications role.

Living / in touch as of 2026. Not to be confused with Ernie Sutcliffe.
