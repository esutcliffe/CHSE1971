Ernest Leonard Rheaume

Ernest Leonard Rheaume — known as Ernie — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Bolton Street in Cranston. In high school he was in the National Honor Society, U.R.I. Chemistry Seminars, and R.I.C. History Colloquia.

He is married to Jeanne (DiPrete) Rheaume, Cranston West Class of 1973. Their children are Ernest Roger (“Ernie”), who was living in Colorado by 2014 and is married to Kim; Nicole, of Massachusetts, married to Ben Scheff; and Christina, who was living in Spain as of 2014.

He has worked as Director of Software Services at Hasbro in Cranston, and holds an MBA and an M.S. in Finance from the University of Rhode Island. He has been a steady class volunteer. He has also been involved with Boy Scouting as an adult, including serving as master of ceremonies at a local Eagle Court of Honor in June 2022.

Living / in touch as of 2026. Not to be confused with Ernie Sutcliffe or with other Rheaumes connected to Hasbro or URI.
