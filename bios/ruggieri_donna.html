Donna Marie (Ruggieri) Fresnilla, Class of 1971

Donna Marie Ruggieri grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Salvatore and Albertine (Leveille) Ruggieri.

At Cranston High School East, Donna was advertising manager for the Thunderbolt three years running, as a sophomore, junior and senior. She cheered with the Pep Squad as a sophomore, joined the Italian Club as a junior, and took part in Football Follies as a junior and senior. As a senior she added the Leader Corps, the Ski Club and the Class Council, where she also served as an alternate.

After high school Donna, who became Donna Fresnilla, stayed close to home in Rhode Island for many years, living in Coventry, West Warwick and North Kingstown. Her husband may be Peter Fresnilla. Public records show the two of them living at the same addresses, though we haven’t found a marriage record. Around 2015 she moved south to the Port Charlotte and Punta Gorda area on Florida’s Gulf Coast, and around 2017 Florida corporate records listed her as a director of Homeowners of Port Charlotte Village, her neighborhood’s homeowners’ association.

Donna is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach her, please let us know.
