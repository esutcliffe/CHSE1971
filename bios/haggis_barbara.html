Barbara Marie (Haggis) Thompson

Barbara Haggis grew up in Cranston, the daughter of William L. and Barbara S. (Mackewicz) Haggis. Her father was an installer for the New England Telephone Company and lived in Cranston for 45 years. Her mother, who was born in Providence, worked for the Coro Jewelry Company for many years before retiring and was a member of the St. Paul's Women's Club in Cranston. At Cranston East Barbara was a cheerleader for three years and served on the Student Council for three years, including a term as secretary in her junior year. As a senior she was chairman of Cogitari and served as Student Representative to the Faculty Advisory Board. She sang in the A Cappella Choir for three years and also worked on the Ways and Means and Social committees, Football Follies and the Foster Parents Committee.

On November 16, 1974, Barbara married Robert Edmond Thompson of South Dennis, Massachusetts, at Meshanticut Park Baptist Church in Cranston, with a reception at the Heathstone Motor Inn in Seekonk. By 1986 she was living in Saunderstown, and she and Robert were still making their home there in 2007. Barbara has stayed in touch with the class. At the 50th reunion she was photographed with Dan Barry, Domenic D'Antuono, Pat Giarrusso and Maryann Pezza Matano, and with Maryann Pezza Matano and Lauren Goding Johnson.
