Gail H. (Cedar) Heymsfield

Gail Cedar grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Louis and Evelyn (Goodman) Cedar. At Cranston East she was a member of the National Honor Society as a junior and senior, and she was one of the class's all-round athletes, playing softball as a junior and field hockey, tennis and volleyball as a senior. She also belonged to the Folk Workshop as a junior and the French Club as a senior.

Gail married and became Gail Heymsfield, and by 2000 she was living in Gaithersburg, Maryland. A Gail Heymsfield of nearby North Potomac ran the TLC King of the Road 5K in Rockville in 2012, which may well have been her. She is one of the classmates the reunion committee has been trying to find since at least 2011. If you know how to reach Gail, please let us know.
