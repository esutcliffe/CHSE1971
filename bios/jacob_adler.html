Jacob Adler

Jacob Adler graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Norfolk Street in Cranston. In high school he was concertmaster, National Honor Society, Math Team, Ecology Action, Orchestra, Charisma staff, Thyrsus Club, Russian Club, Civic Leadership, and Science Club. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives he was named Outstanding Student and Most Likely to Succeed (both with Jessica Morris).

After Harvard (A.B. in philosophy and mathematics, 1976; Ph.D. in philosophy, 1985), he joined the University of Arkansas philosophy faculty in 1984 and later helped found Jewish Studies there. He became a lay leader at Temple Shalom of Northwest Arkansas, studied at the Reconstructionist Rabbinical College, and was ordained in 2006 as the congregation’s first resident rabbi. He later retired from that pulpit (public accounts place the end around 2019 or 2020) and is Temple Shalom’s rabbi emeritus. As of 2023–2025 he was still teaching philosophy and Jewish studies at the University of Arkansas and leading Minyan on the Hill, a traditional-egalitarian prayer group in Fayetteville — including inviting worshippers to Yom Kippur services in September 2025.

He is married to Hayya Knopf. Their children are Luu (Mordechai Yitzchak Chanah) and Lily (Esther Rachel Orit).
