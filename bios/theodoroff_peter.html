Peter Theodoroff

Peter Theodoroff grew up in Cranston and graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971.

Pete has stayed in touch with the class. On September 13, 2003, he joined about 125 classmates at the Hotel Viking in Newport for the class's "5-0 Birthday Party," our 32nd reunion. In his write-up of the night, Ed DeJesus thanked everyone who came "from great distances," adding, "I think Peter Theodoroff was the winner there."
