Donald Matthew Aquilante

Donald Matthew Aquilante graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Hyde Street in Cranston. In high school he was DECA, Ampersand, Football Club, and J.V. Football.

Class notes in 2011–2013 knew him as a 40th-reunion attendee with his wife of (then) 36 years, proud of a daughter at Johnson & Wales in hotel/hospitality studies, and in regular touch with classmate Ernie Rheaume through Boy Scout planning. Soft family note: daughter-in-law Michelle Aquilante died November 2013.
