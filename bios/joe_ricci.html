Joseph Alfred Ricci

Joseph Alfred Ricci — known as Joe — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Wayland Avenue in Cranston. In high school he was National Honor Society, Class Council, Math Team, Band (treasurer), Orchestra, Thyrsus Club, URI Science Seminars, Calendar Committee, and Football Follies.

He has lived in Maryland since 1976. He and his wife Priscilla raised two children, Liz (Lizz / Elizabeth) and Chris (Christopher), and make their home in Abingdon. He spent about thirty years in federal environmental engineering for the Army, then retired from that career around early 2008 and moved into workforce work he enjoyed in a small office, including a long stretch with the Susquehanna Workforce Network in Havre de Grace. He has stayed visible at reunions — including photos at the 32nd and bocce with Ed DeJesus at the 35th. Class notes in 2011–2013 celebrated daughter Lizz’s museum work (a MUSE Award video, an Examiner.com blog, then operations manager at the Havre de Grace Maritime Museum) and noted Joe’s Groucho Marx turn at a Harford Leadership Academy fundraiser. His mother, Anna D. Ricci of Cranston, died in January 2017.
