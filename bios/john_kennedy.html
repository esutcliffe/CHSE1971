John Stephen Kennedy

John Stephen Kennedy graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Waite Avenue in Cranston. In high school he was Student Council (vice-president senior year), Class Council, Citizenship Committee, Basketball, Basketball Club, Governor’s Youth Advisory Council, and Extended School Year Committee (student representative).

Class notes later counted him among the class’s serious runners (clustered with Fowler and Caldarola-Smith) and recorded his Park View hoops memories of Marc Plante — and of Marc’s brother Freddy as a Rhode Island schoolyard basketball legend. He appears on the 32nd-reunion phone-caller list with Lauren Goding Johnson and others.
