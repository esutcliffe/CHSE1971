Cosima Joan Fallon

Cosima Fallon grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she played in the Band for three years and the Orchestra as a senior, sang in the Chorus, and was a member of Charisma and the Track Club.

Cosima has remained one of the class's connectors. She helped with last-minute details for the 32nd reunion, the "5-O Birthday Party" on September 13, 2003, and some of the photos from that night on the reunion pages are hers. She was also on the team that worked behind the scenes on the 40th reunion in 2011. In those years she made phone calls to track down classmates, some of them with Franne Donovan, and helped at check-in. Cosima is proud of her brother Dave Fallon's long radio career, which earned him a place in the Rhode Island Radio Hall of Fame in 2014. She lives in Cranston.
