Brian John McMahon

At Cranston East, Brian McMahon was a mainstay of the Thyrsus Club, the school’s drama club, for three years, and served as its vice president as a junior. He was also on the Assembly Committee and took part in Civic Leadership, the Citizenship Committee and the German Club.

Brian and Diane McMahon came to the class’s 32nd reunion in 2003 and signed up for the 40th reunion in 2011. We haven’t been able to find more about Brian’s life since then. If you know how to reach him, please let us know.
