Deborah Anne Kopech

Deborah Kopech grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Albert and Anne C. "Nancy" (Ryan) Kopech. Her father, who was born in Rozan, Poland, was a stockbroker with Schneider Securities in Providence for many years and lived in Cranston for 37 years. Her mother was born in Providence and lived in Cranston most of her life. At Cranston East Deborah was a member of the Thyrsus Club for three years and served on the Calendar Committee as a sophomore, junior and senior. She was on the Assembly Committee and in the Russian Club in her junior and senior years, and she was on the Thunderbolt staff as a sophomore and a senior. A member of Ecology Action as a junior and senior, she was its secretary in her senior year. She also sang in the Mixed Chorus as a sophomore and was a Class Council alternate as a junior. As a senior she joined the Leader Corps and the Foster Parents Committee and appeared in the Cranston Follies.

After East, Deborah earned a bachelor's degree in history and English at Drew University in Madison, New Jersey, in 1975. She spent fifteen years as a senior director at Sallie Mae, from 1990 to 2005, and later worked in finance and human resources for the Westerly Public Schools, retiring in 2016. She lives in Narragansett, Rhode Island, where she was elected to the Town Council and re-elected in 2022. She is on our list of missing classmates.
