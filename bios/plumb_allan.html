Allan Watson Plumb, Class of 1971

Allan Watson Plumb grew up in Edgewood, the son of Ruth H. (Watson) Plumb, a licensed practical nurse at the Scandinavian Home in Cranston, and his middle name came from her side of the family.

At Cranston High School East, Allan was one of our musicians. He played in the Band for three years and in the Orchestra and the German Band as a junior and senior, and he performed at the Solo and Ensemble Festival in each of his three years. He swam for East as a junior, and he was part of the Junior Talent Show and two years of Football Follies.

When his mother died in 2003, her obituary named her two sons, Allan W. Plumb of Newburgh, New York, and Donald L. Plumb of Warwick.

We believe that Allan carried his music a long way from Edgewood. Newburgh sits just up the Hudson from West Point, and a clarinetist named Allan Plumb served there as a staff sergeant in the United States Military Academy Band. In April 1989 he and bassoonist Karl Balandovich were featured in “An Evening of Woodwind Chamber Music” at the post chapel, and in January 1990 he played with the Candlelight Woodwind Quintet. He is listed in the clarinet section on the band’s 1993 album, recorded between 1991 and 1993. By 2007 the newspapers called him a “retired West Point Band member,” and that October he came back to the post to play Mendelssohn’s Concert Piece No. 2 for two clarinets and piano with his former section-mate, Staff Sgt. Jeffrey Geller. In 2014 an Allan Plumb was still playing clarinet, in a woodwind quintet with the SUNY Orange Chamber Ensemble in Middletown. None of these notices mentions Cranston, so we can’t be certain he is our classmate, but the town, the instrument, and the boy who never missed a band season at East make us think he is.

If you are Allan, or you know where he is today, we would love to hear from you.
