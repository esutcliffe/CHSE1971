Austin George Livesey, Class of 1971

Austin George Livesey grew up in the Eden Park section of Cranston, the son of the late Austin G. Livesey Jr. and Dorothy (Hovanick) Livesey.

At Cranston High School East, Austin was a distance runner. He ran cross country and indoor track for three years and outdoor track as a junior and senior, and he belonged to the Track Club as a junior. He was just as strong in the classroom, earning a place in the National Honor Society in his junior and senior years. As a sophomore he also played baseball and was a member of the Chess Club.

Today Austin and his wife, Carol, live in Coventry, not far from the Cranston neighborhood where he grew up.

Austin is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach him, please let us know.
