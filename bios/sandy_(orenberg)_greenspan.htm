Sandy's note to the class, 2018:

Hi

I'm Sandy Greenspan (Sandy Orenberg) and I live in the Berkshires. I found my name on the list of lost classmates, but I'm not lost.

I am the controller for a group of companies that own radio stations, an internet company and a cellular tower company.

My significant other (John) and our dog and I are restoring an old 1700's home and are planning to run a bed and breakfast in a few years.

I don't know that I'll be able to make the reunion this time but "Hi to everyone".

Sandy

Compiled October 2026

Sandra Robin Orenberg graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. At East she was in the National Honor Society in her junior and senior years, served on the Cranstonian editorial staff as a senior, and was vice president of the French Club. She also sang in Mixed Chorus and took part in Charisma, Thunderbolt, the Spanish Club, and Future Teachers.

She is the daughter of George Orenberg, who died in 1996, and Gertrude (Levine) Orenberg (1926–2007), who taught first grade in Scituate for thirty years (Gertrude's obituary).

The companies in her note were those of Aritaur Communications, where she had been controller since about 2000. Its holdings included Berkshire and Martha's Vineyard radio stations, a tower business, and Martha's Vineyard Online.

Sandy met John F. Kiley in 1981. They bought and renovated a small home in Housatonic in 1993, and in 2001 they moved to the Glendale section of Stockbridge, where they restored the older home she mentions in her note. John died at their Stockbridge home on February 10, 2019 (John's obituary). Sandy lives in Stockbridge.
