Francesca Nottie Shull, Class of 1971

Francesca Madeline Nottie grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Joseph T. Nottie Jr. and Victoria F. Agnoli, both born in Providence. She was likely named for her grandmother, Francesca Ricci Nottie. The family were communicants of St. Mary's Church in Cranston.

Her father served in the Army infantry in World War II, in Africa and Europe, earning three Bronze Stars and a Purple Heart, and later was a grand knight of the Knights of Columbus Leo Council. Her mother, a longtime Cranston resident, kept the books for Ruggieri Brothers Flooring for many years and belonged to the Knightsville Library Association and the St. Mary's Feast Society Ladies Auxiliary.

Francesca graduated from Cranston High School East in 1971 and from Providence College in 1975, and went on to study at the Bentley Institute of Paralegal Studies.

Now married to Wayne Shull, Francesca has lived in both Texas and Rhode Island. Her father died in November 2004 and was buried with military honors at St. Ann Cemetery; her mother died in October 2014.
