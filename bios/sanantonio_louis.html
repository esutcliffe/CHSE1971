Louis A. SanAntonio, Class of 1971

Louis A. SanAntonio grew up in Cranston, the son of Louis and Gloria (Piccirilli) San Antonio. At Cranston High School East he wrestled in his junior year.

Louis has stayed connected with his classmates over the years. He appears in photographs from a 2003 class gathering, was registered for the 40th reunion in 2011, and attended the 55th reunion in 2026 with his wife, April.

Research note: A 1995 U.S. Copyright Office registration, "A collection of songs by Louis A. SanAntonio: vol. 1," lists a claimant of the same name born in 1952. This record has not been linked to our classmate, and we include it only as an open question; if you can confirm or rule out the connection, please let us know.
