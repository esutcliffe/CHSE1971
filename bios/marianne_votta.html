Marianne Catherine Votta

Marianne Catherine Votta graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Eden Crest Drive in Cranston. In high school she was Student Council, Class Council, Dean’s Council (Program Chairman senior year), Social Committee, Color Guard, National Honor Society, Wrestling Club, Spanish Club, and Ski Club.
