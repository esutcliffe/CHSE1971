Monika M. (Szynkarski) Curnett

Monika M. Szynkarski — known as Monika (Szynkarski) Curnett — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Sunset Terrace in Cranston. In high school she was Class Council Secretary, Thyrsus Club, German Club, Future Teachers, Mixed Chorus, Sunshine Committee chair, Junior Talent Show, and Cranstonian homeroom representative.

Classmates following Ed’s updates knew her as a retired speech-language pathologist (30 years as of an April 2009 note) who traveled widely with Nancy Goldis Iacono and the Iaconos — Middle East, Costa Rica, Turkey with a Cappadocia balloon ride. On the class site she appears in 32nd- and 35th-reunion photos and was thanked for handling 35th-reunion money. Soft, dated: only child Ross a Doctor of Physical Therapy and clinic director in Charleston (Ed 2009).

Living / in touch as of last class data. Exact birth date not found.
