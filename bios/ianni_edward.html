Edward Frank “Ed” Ianni, Class of 1971

At Cranston High School East, Ed Ianni’s interests included both the ice and the camera: he played hockey and joined the Hockey Club as a sophomore and junior, and took part in the Photography Club as a sophomore and senior.

In the years after graduation, Ed made a place for himself in Rhode Island’s bowling community. The Rhode Island United States Bowling Congress association’s 2011–2012 directory lists him as an association director, and in 2024 the association recognized him in its Hall of Fame for Meritorious Service.

He also helped bring old classmates back together. Ed was among the volunteers thanked for helping with the Class of 1971’s 35th reunion at Yawgoo Bakes in September 2006.
