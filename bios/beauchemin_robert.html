Robert Beauchemin

Bob Beauchemin grew up in Cranston and was one of the busiest athletes and class leaders at Cranston East. He played soccer as a junior and senior and captained the team in his senior year, belonged to the Soccer Club, and wrestled as a sophomore and a senior. He served on the Class Council as a junior and senior, sat on the Social Committee and skied with the Ski Club for three years, and performed in the Talent Show as a junior.

Bob has stayed close to the class. He came to the 32nd reunion, the "5-O Birthday Party" on September 13, 2003, where he was photographed with classmates Dianne Centofante Miller, Pat Giarrusso and Debi Zannella. He was back for the 50th reunion at the Bonnet Shores Beach Club in Narragansett on June 25, 2022, where he posed with Pat Giarrusso, Maryann Pezza Matano, Ed DeJesus, Pat Dewey Giarrusso and other classmates in front of the Cranston East Thunderbolts banner. He lives in Cranston.
