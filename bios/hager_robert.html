Robert Edward Hager, Class of 1971

Robert Edward Hager grew up in the Edgewood section of Cranston, the son of the late Dr. Herbert F. Hager, longtime physician for the Providence School Department, and Marion (Hammer) Hager.

At Cranston High School East, Robert was one of the people who made the shows happen. He belonged to the Thyrsus Club for three years and worked behind the scenes as stage manager and as lights production chairman in his junior and senior years, and he was part of the Football Follies both of those years too. He served on the Citizenship Committee for three years, chairing it as a junior, and he was a member of the Ski Club and the A.V.A.

After graduation Robert followed his father to Bates College in Lewiston, Maine, where he was a member of the Class of 1975. He later came home to Cranston, and for many years he kept the family house in Edgewood, until about 2013.

Robert is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach him, please let us know.
