Kathleen “Kathie” (Mooney) Gouveia

Kathleen Mooney graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up in the Edgewood section of Cranston. At East she was the cartoonist for the Thunderbolt, served on the Cranstonian advertising staff, the Ways and Means Committee, and the Red Cross Council, and was in the National Honor Society. She was also in the Hockey, Spanish, and Thyrsus Clubs.

Kathie is one of six children of Raymond L. and Barbara M. (Meanerhan) Mooney. Her brother Tom died in 2007 (obituary), and her brother Mark died in 2010 (obituary). Both obituaries list her as Kathleen Gouveia of East Providence.

Kathie taught art in the East Providence schools, including at Kent Heights Elementary (2011 staff directory). She retired from Martin Middle School in 2021, when colleagues surprised her with a drive-by salute at her home (East Providence Reporter).

Kathie attended the 2003 reunion, where she was photographed with her classmate Helen (Desmond) McDonald.
