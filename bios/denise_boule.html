Denise M. Boulé

Denise M. Boulé graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Oakland Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was Class Council vice-president, Social Committee, Citizenship Committee, AFS Committee, Hockey Club, Junior Talent Show chair, and Folk Show. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Ideal Cranstonite (with Daniel Barry).

The class site already carries a May 2003 Providence Journal Edwatch column on high-school advisories that features her as principal of Exeter–West Greenwich High School and describes her, in the article’s words, as that year’s principal of the year. Soft: that phrasing is the column’s; it is not the same as the House elementary “Rhode Island Principal of the Year” named the same week. Public career notes sketch a long Rhode Island path from classroom history teaching through assistant principal and high-school principalships, later mentoring and higher-ed lecturing.
