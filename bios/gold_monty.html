Monty Edward Gold, Class of 1971

Monty Edward Gold grew up in Cranston, the son of Victor Gold and Mrs. Victor Gold. For 13 years his father ran Victor Gold & Sons, a wiping-cloth business in Warwick. Monty graduated from Cranston High School East in June 1971.

On April 1, 1978, Monty married Anna Kolodner, daughter of Dr. and Mrs. Berko Kolodner of Brookline, at Kehillath Israel in Brookline. Anna is a graduate of Boston University and holds a master's degree from Boston College. The couple made their home in the Boston area, where Monty appears to have made a career in real estate.

Monty lost his father in January 1985.
