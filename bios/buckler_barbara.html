Barbara Joan (Buckler) Shapiro

Barbara Buckler grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Samuel and Laura (Kagan) Buckler. At Cranston East she was one of the class's busiest students: a member of the National Honor Society and the Citizenship Committee as a junior and senior, in the Future Teachers Club for three years (historian as a junior), manager of the baseball team for three seasons, and a member of the Red Cross Council, the Ways and Means Committee and the Hockey Club as a junior and the Spanish Club as a senior.

Barbara married Nathan Shapiro, and the couple make their home in West Warwick. She came to the class's 32nd reunion, the "5-O Birthday Party" on September 13, 2003, where she was photographed with Patti Nulman Simpson, Beverly Caldarola McFarland, Jeff Whitfield, Paul Varadian and other classmates, and she had her check in early for the 40th reunion at the Bonnet Shores Beach Club in Narragansett on June 10, 2011. Her father, Samuel Buckler, a World War II Army Air Forces veteran who co-founded United Printing and lived in Cranston for more than sixty years, died in 2023 at 99. Her mother, Laura, died in 1999.
