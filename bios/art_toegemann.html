Arthur “Art” Toegemann

Arthur “Art” Toegemann graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. (No senior portrait or address/activities block in the slim yearbook file.)

He is a Providence-area songwriter who helped host the Mediator Stage open mic at the Church of the Mediator (50 Rounds Avenue) with Don Tassone and Frank Martyn — coverage that reached *American Songwriter*. Class notes remember him on the Mediator coffee-house circuit and filming the 2013 birthday party. Soft public trail: Vietnam-era protest songs and VVAW letters under Arthur J. Toegemann.
