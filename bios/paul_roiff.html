Paul Gary Roiff

Paul Gary Roiff grew up in Cranston, Rhode Island, and graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. The yearbook listed his home as 41 Brookside Drive. He is a Rhode Island native who has long made his professional life in the Boston area.

He is the son of Herbert Allen Roiff and Miriam Irene (Wilk) Roiff, both Providence-born; they later lived in Lantana, Florida, and died there in 2018 and 2019. His sisters are Marsha (Roiff) Davidson (CHSE 1968) of Avon, Connecticut, and Mindy (Roiff) Shanfeld of Mount Pleasant, South Carolina. His wife is Kristin of Brookline, Massachusetts; a September 2012 wedding at Clarendon Court in Newport is noted in public photo captions. Family accounts also point to a daughter, Dr. Tracey Roiff, though that link is not confirmed in vital records reviewed here.

Company and press bios credit him with a bachelor’s degree and an MBA from Boston University and a J.D. from Suffolk University Law School. As president of Heath Properties / Heath Management Co. in Boston, he partnered with Jamie Mammano and Seth Greenberg (Columbus Hospitality) on a run of well-known Massachusetts restaurants and hotels: Mistral (1997), XV Beacon and its restaurant (later Mooo), Teatro, Sorellina, L’Andana in Burlington, the Inn at St. Botolph, and Ostra. Other public chapters include the East Boston Loftel project (sold in 2017), the purchase and later sale of Clarendon Court in Newport (2012–2021), and a 2014 bid for Newport Grand that did not close on the original terms.
