Carmen Troiano Cavanaugh

Carmen Anne Troiano — now Carmen Troiano Cavanaugh — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Narragansett Boulevard in Cranston. In high school she was Student Council, Class Council, Social Committee (chair junior year), Athletic Committee (chair senior year), Ways and Means, Leader Corps, G.A.A. (secretary, vice president, then president senior year), and Red Cross Council. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Most Athletic (with Timothy Harrington).
