Andrew Marc Gilstein, Class of 1971

Andrew Marc Gilstein grew up in Cranston, the son of Benjamin and Lillian (Krakowsky) Gilstein. At Cranston High School East, his activities ranged from football and track to chess, the National Honor Society and Model Legislature. He also attended the University of Rhode Island’s Saturday Seminars.

Andrew continued his education at URI, graduating in 1975 with a bachelor’s degree in political science, with distinction. He earned his law degree from Albany Law School in 1978.

On January 11, 1981, the Providence Journal announced his engagement to Sharon Beth Resnick, daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Earl J. Resnick of Cranston. Sharon had attended Emerson College and was then attending URI. They later married.

Andrew built his legal career in Rhode Island. His work has included business and corporate law, commercial and residential real estate, estate planning, probate and tax matters. He has also advised nonprofit organizations, including religious organizations and social-service agencies, and lectured on estate planning and related subjects.

A founding partner of Gilstein, Kinder & Levin, Andrew became a partner at Pannone Devereaux & O’Gara when the firms joined in September 2026. The announcement marks a change of firm, not retirement.

His community involvement has included the Touro Fraternal Association. In 2023, its newsletter recognized him after twelve years of service on its board of directors. His professional biography also records service as president of Touro’s Friendship Lodge.
