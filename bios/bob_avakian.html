Robert Michael “Bob” Avakian

Robert Michael “Bob” Avakian graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Mauran Street in Cranston. In high school he was Thyrsus Club, Charisma (Chairman senior year), and Class Council Vice-President sophomore year.

He appears on the 32nd-reunion thank-you list as Bob Avakian.
