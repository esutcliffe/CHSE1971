Jo-Ann Louise (Faubert) Junod

Jo-Ann Faubert grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she was a member of the Thyrsus Club and the Future Teachers Club for three years, from sophomore through senior year. She was in the Debating Club as a sophomore and on the Play Reading Committee as a junior and senior. The class lists give her married name as Junod.

Her parents were Edward and Irene R. (Durant) Faubert. Irene lived in Cranston for many years, graduated from the Roger Williams General Hospital School of Nursing in 1950, and volunteered on eleven Operation Smile missions. She died on February 9, 2025, at the age of 95. Her obituary names her daughter, Jo-Ann Junod of Warwick.

Jo-Ann’s husband, Charles F. Junod III, was born in New Rochelle, New York. He earned a bachelor’s degree at Knox College and a master’s at Georgetown, served as a first lieutenant in Army intelligence during the Vietnam War, and later worked in human resources and sales management for General Electric. His obituary says he and Jo-Ann had been married for about 30 years. He died on October 7, 2022, at the age of 79, and a memorial service was held in Warwick on November 26, 2022.

She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Jo-Ann, please let us know.
