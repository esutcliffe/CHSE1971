Catherine Ann (Rainone) DiPietro

Cathy Rainone grew up in Cranston. Providence Journal obituaries from 1992 and 2002 name her parents as Anthony Rainone, a truck driver for the M.A. Gammino Construction Co. for 44 years, and Carmella (DeLuca) Rainone, both of Cranston. Those obituaries also list a daughter, Cathy or Catherine DiPietro of Cumberland, which gives her married name. She was living in Cumberland as of 2002.

At Cranston East Cathy was in the Leader Corps as a junior and senior and was its Commander as a senior. She belonged to the Italian Club as a junior and senior and was its Vice President as a junior. She served on the Junior Class Publicity Committee and took part in Football Follies as a junior and senior, as Co-Chairman as a junior. She sat on the Social Committee as a junior and senior and was part of the Thunderbolt as a junior. She also took part in the Girls’ Athletic Association for three years.
