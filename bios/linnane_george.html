George W. Linnane Jr., Class of 1971

George W. Linnane Jr. was born in October 1952 and grew up in Cranston in a family of educators. His parents, George W. Linnane Sr. and Dorine E. (Laudati) Linnane, were both on the Cranston High faculty as early as 1940. His father, a Boston University graduate and World War II Army veteran, spent 26 years as a teacher and administrator in the Cranston schools and retired in 1977 as a high school counselor in Lincoln. In retirement he became a published outdoor and travel photographer. His mother, a Brown graduate, taught math and later library science in the Cranston schools for 18 years. The family were communicants of Immaculate Conception Church in Cranston. George graduated from Cranston High School East in June 1971.

George has made his home in North Kingstown since about 1983. He works as a real estate agent in Narragansett, and he appears to have served several terms on North Kingstown's Leisure Services Advisory Committee.

George lost his father in 1995 and his mother in May 2003.
