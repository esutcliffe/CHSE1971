Compiled October 2026

Elaine Marie Buck

Elaine Buck was a member of the Class of 1971 at Cranston East. She was in the Color Guard as a junior and senior and served on the Red Cross Council in those two years, as its Treasurer as a senior. She was in the Future Nurses Club as a sophomore and junior and its Treasurer as a junior. She sang in the Mixed Chorus for three years, from sophomore through senior year. She was also in Thyrsus and the German Club as a sophomore and junior, and in the Future Teachers Club as a junior. As a senior she also took part in the Foster Parents Committee, Modern Dance and the Wrestling Club.

We believe, though we have not confirmed it, that Elaine’s mother was Marie (Wirth) Buck and her father was John D. Buck, and that she married Richard E. Gallo. This comes from Providence Journal and funeral-home obituaries from 2017 and 2023, which name an Elaine Gallo and her husband Richard of Smithfield. They give her family only by those names; none of them says she attended Cranston East, and our class lists pair the names Buck and Gallo. If this is our Elaine, she and Richard were living in Smithfield in 2023.

She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Elaine, please let us know.
