F. Donald “Don” McKendall III, Class of 1971

F. Donald “Don” McKendall III grew up in Cranston, the son of Frank D. and Anna (Kuczinski) McKendall.

At Cranston High School East, Don swam as a junior and senior, ran outdoor track all three years, played football as a sophomore, and belonged to the Hockey Club as a junior and senior. He served on Class Council all three years, took part in the Junior Talent Show and the Assembly Committee as a junior, and joined DECA as a senior.

Don met classmate Mariacristina C. “Tina” DeMarco in a chemistry class at East, where, as Tina would proudly tell it, he jumped in to light her Bunsen burner. The two walked home from school together, and Don carried her books. He never quite got over how many she had. They married at St. Matthew Church in Cranston in 1983, moved to North Kingstown, and raised three children. They have two grandsons.

In 1999, Don and Tina launched SERVPRO cleanup and restoration franchises. Don is president and treasurer of McKendall & Associates, Inc. (SERVPRO of Northern Rhode Island), and he owns three franchises, including two in Massachusetts.

Don has stayed close to the class. He and Tina came to the 32nd reunion in 2003, and they were among the planners of the 40th reunion.

Tina died on June 24, 2026, and is buried at St. Ann Cemetery in Cranston. The class already has a memorial page for her: Mariacristina DeMarco (McKendall).
