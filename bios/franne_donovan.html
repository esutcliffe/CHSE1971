Frances Jane Donovan

Frances Jane Donovan — known as Franne — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Orlando Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was National Honor Society, Thyrsus Club, Modern Dance, Class Council, Charisma, and Italian Club.

She married Jeff Amelse, a chemical engineer from Chicago, and raised two children, Ben and Caroline (Cari), in the far west suburbs of Chicago, with a career in health programs and insurance. Classmates remember her as a steady reunion phone volunteer in the early 2010s — helping plan the 2011 reunion, sharing Ken Rogers’s memorial details, and alerting classmates when Ronnie Stabile’s health turned critical. In 2012 daughter Caroline was named to Mortar Board at the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa. As of February 2020 she and Jeffrey were of Batavia, Illinois.

Soft family notes: her older brother Father James W. Donovan, a Paulist (CSP) priest long associated with San Francisco’s Chinatown ministry, died suddenly in October 2010; her brother Edward “Ted” Donovan Sr. died in February 2020.
