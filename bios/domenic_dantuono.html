Domenic A. D’Antuono

Domenic A. D’Antuono graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Mica Avenue in Cranston. In high school he was Basketball, Football, Basketball Club (vice president junior year), and Future Farmers. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives he was named Best Looking (with Debra Feldman).

He turns up often in class reunion albums: captioned at the 32nd (including the playful “Silver Fox, Domenic Holding Court”), thanked among the 40th-reunion helpers, and nametag-confirmed at the 50th (June 2022).
