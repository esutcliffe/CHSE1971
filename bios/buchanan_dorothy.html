Dorothy Ann (Buchanan) Rocchio

Dorothy Buchanan grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she was a member of the Ski Club for three years and the Hockey Club as a junior and senior. As a junior she served on the Cranstonian advertising staff, and as a senior she joined Leader Corps and took part in the Football Follies.

On June 30, 1979, Dorothy married Robert A. Rocchio of Providence. The ceremony and a garden reception took place at her mother's home in North Kingstown, her brother gave her in marriage, and her sister was matron of honor. The couple planned to make their home in Cranston. Robert worked in construction for Cardi Corp., was a member of Teamsters Local 251, and served in the Army National Guard. He died on January 20, 2001. She is one of the classmates the reunion committee has been trying to find since at least 2011. If you know how to reach Dorothy, please let us know.
