Garry Timothy McDonough

Garry Timothy McDonough graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Peerless Street in Cranston. In high school he was Wrestling, Wrestling Club, Football, Football Club, Baseball, and Class Council alternate. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives he was named Most Humorous (with Helen Dittelman). Spell Garry.
