David Deverell, Class of 1971

David Roy Deverell grew up in Cranston, the only child of Roy I. Deverell and Florence Babcock. His father, born in Providence, was a Navy veteran of World War II who survived the sinking of the destroyer Emmons, and later worked as a hub and die cutter at Craftsman Tool & Die. Roy was a deacon of Phillips Memorial Baptist Church in Cranston, where David was a member too, and he gave years to scouting, earning the Scout Oscar, a district award of merit and the Capt. George Bucklin medal of honor. David's mother worked as a carder at Bay Sales in Cranston and earlier at Argonne Jewelry.

At Cranston High School East, David played tennis for three years, wrestled and ran cross country as a sophomore, and joined the Gymnastics Club as a senior. He graduated in 1971 and came back for the class's 2003 reunion.

His father died in December 1975 and his mother in September 1994; both are buried at Highland Memorial Park in Johnston. David is the father of two sons.
