Rhonda Debra (Port) Walker

Rhonda Port grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Seymour and Bernice (Kaufman) Port. Her father, a Brown University graduate, was a self-employed manufacturers' representative in the costume-jewelry business, and he lived in Cranston for about 60 years. At Cranston East Rhonda was one of the most active students in the class. She was a member of the National Honor Society as a junior and senior, and as a senior she served on the Student Council and the Cranstonian editorial staff. She belonged to the Foster Parents Committee for three years and was its chairman as a senior. She was also in Future Teachers for three years, tutored as a junior, attended the U.R.I. Chemistry Seminars as a sophomore, was program chairman of the Spanish Club as a junior, and belonged to the French Club as a sophomore.

Rhonda went on to Brown University and became an actuary. She joined Metropolitan Property and Liability Insurance as a summer intern in 1974, became a Fellow of the Casualty Actuarial Society, and was named a senior director there in 1987. On May 15, 1988, she married Roger Walker at the Holiday Inn at the Crossing in Warwick, and the couple made their home in New Jersey. Rhonda has stayed connected to the class. She attended the 25th reunion at the Westin in 1996, and for the 32nd reunion she was one of the classmates who phoned people around the country to track down missing classmates.
