Shelley L. (Brough) Mingus

Shelley Brough grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Adam J. and Evelyn C. (Petrucci) Brough. The family moved to Cranston in 1951. Her father was a self-employed interior decorator for 25 years and a member of the Painters and Decorators Union. At Cranston East Shelley was a member of the National Honor Society in her junior and senior years and served on its Social Committee as a senior. She took Office Practice as a junior and senior, worked on the Calendar Committee as a senior and was in Civic Leadership as a sophomore.

Shelley later made her home in Houston, Texas, where she was living as Shelley L. Mingus in 1989. She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Shelley, please let us know.
