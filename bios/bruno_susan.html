Susan Mary (Bruno) Sovis

Sue Bruno grew up in Cranston, a daughter of Atlanto and Joanne Bruno. At Cranston East she sang in the Mixed Chorus as a sophomore, and as a senior she took Office Practice and served on the Calendar Committee. She later married and took the name Sovis.

Sue gave her time to the class's 32nd reunion, the "5-O Birthday Party" on September 13, 2003. She was one of the volunteers who called "hundreds of complete strangers all over the country" to track down missing classmates, and they found many. Her mother, Joanne Bruno, died in Cranston in December 2016 at the age of 88. The class has since lost touch with Sue. If you know how to reach her, please let us know.
