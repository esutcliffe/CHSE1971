Donna Kempf Johnson, Class of 1971

Donna Marie Kempf grew up in the Edgewood section of Cranston, the daughter of John J. and Marilyn (Cronin) Kempf, and the family attended St. Paul Church.

At Cranston High School East, Donna was in the thick of things. She managed the football team, served as an attendance officer and in the Leader Corps as a senior, belonged to the Hockey Club for two years and the Italian Club as a sophomore, and took the stage in the Junior Talent Show, the Football Follies and the Cranston Follies. She graduated in 1971.

In the fall of 1972 Donna became engaged to David P. Johnson, a fellow Cranston East graduate. They married on May 12, 1973, with the Rev. Richard A. Donnelly officiating and her brothers Jack and James serving as ushers, followed by a reception at the Palestine Shrine in Cranston and a honeymoon in Williamsburg, Virginia. Donna and David are still married, raised three children and have grandchildren, and they still make their home in Cranston.

She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Donna, please let us know.
