Christine Mary Altieri

Christine Mary Altieri graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Knollwood Avenue in Cranston. In high school she was Dean’s Council, Thunderbolt Homeroom Representative, Thyrsus Club, Cranstonian Homeroom Representative, Charisma Prose Staff, Great Books Club, A Cappella Choir, National Honor Society, Social Committee, and R.I.S.D. Film Workshop.
