David Bradford Fowler

David Bradford Fowler — known as Dave — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Dale Avenue in Cranston. In high school he was Cross Country captain, Swimming, Indoor and Outdoor Track, Track Club, and Cranstonian editorial staff and homeroom representative.

As an adult in North Andover, Massachusetts, he became known among classmates as a repeat Boston Marathon runner fundraising for Help in the Nick of Time in memory of his son Nick, blogging the training for years. Class notes in the early 2010s marked his Boston finishes, that he was unhurt in the 2013 bombing and ran again in 2014, daughter Tiffany’s book publication, and son-in-law Marshall Lewy’s film California Solo. His Speakers Corner blog was still posting Marathon Challenge updates into 2025–26.

Soft family notes (dated): mother Grace died December 2012; brother Niles died around 2013. Do not confuse with classmate Gail Fowler. Tiffany’s book is young-adult, not a children’s title.
