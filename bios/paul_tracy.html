Paul Russell Tracy

Paul Russell Tracy graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Doric Avenue in Cranston. In high school he sang in A Cappella Choir and Mixed Chorus, and he was in the National Honor Society, Chess Club, and Thyrsus Club.

A mid-1970s Boston College directory still listed him at a Doric Avenue Cranston address. For many years he has lived in Somerville, Massachusetts, with his wife, Sheila (née Houghton). Their children are Christine and Patrick. His sister Susan Tracy (Cranston East Class of 1972) died in August 2023; her obituary named Paul and Sheila of Somerville among the survivors.

His federal career was as an investigator for the Wage and Hour Division of the U.S. Department of Labor in Boston. Concurrently he served as Executive Vice President of AFGE Local 948, chaired the regional collective-bargaining committee, and was a National Vice President of the National Council of Field Labor Locals (NCFLL) from 1991 to 2001. A mid-1990s NCFLL Courier listed him among top PAC donors and noted Sheila attending a Local 948 labor dinner with him.
