Debra Susan Feldman

Debra Susan Feldman graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Lyndon Road in Cranston. In high school she was Modern Dance, Hockey Club, Social Committee, Future Teachers, Thunderbolt homeroom representative, and Wrestling Club. In the Cranstonian 1971 Senior Superlatives she was named Best Looking (with Domenic D’Antuono).
