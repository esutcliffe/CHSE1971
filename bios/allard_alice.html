Alice Ann (Allard) Greenan, Class of 1971

Alice Ann (Allard) Greenan — known to family and friends as Allie — grew up in Cranston, the daughter of Donald Joseph Allard (1915–1989), a World War II U.S. Army captain, jewelry manufacturing administrator, and award-winning avocational artist, and Alice Linnea (Nelson) Allard (1917–2003). She is one of six Allard children, with four brothers — Robert, Donald, Frederick, and Carl — and a sister, Jean.

The 1971 Cranstonian carries her senior portrait as Alice Ann Allard, with no activities listed.

Alice married fellow Cranston East alumnus David Greenan (Class of 1969); in May 2023 he noted on AlumniClass.com that they had been married 50 years. They have four children and grandchildren — five by 2012, when David mentioned in a Bryant University faculty profile that he liked to spend time with his grandchildren or go for a drive with Allie in their convertible.

She was living in West Warwick at the time of her father's death in 1989, and in Warwick by 2001. In his May 2023 profile, David noted that they split their time between Warwick and Jacksonville, Florida, spending about six months of the year on a houseboat in Florida.

An Alice Greenan served as Interim Operations Director at Community Works Rhode Island, a Providence community-development nonprofit, where she was quoted in a March 2012 press release from U.S. Senator Jack Reed on foreclosure-prevention counseling, and later held a finance-and-operations leadership role there; while the public records we have seen do not explicitly mention her maiden name, this appears to be our classmate.
