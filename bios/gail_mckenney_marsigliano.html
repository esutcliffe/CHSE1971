Gail Ann McKenney Marsigliano

Gail Ann McKenney — later Gail McKenney Marsigliano — grew up in Cranston and graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. In high school she belonged to the Leader Corps as a senior.

Gail remained in Rhode Island, where she has worked as an insurance underwriter; public profiles note her CIIP, ACSR, and CISR insurance designations, and she lists her occupation as underwriter on AlumniClass.com. She was living in Cranston when her brother, Gary “Rabbit” McKenney, died in October 2013, two years after the death of their mother, Lillian McKenney. On AlumniClass.com she lists her interests as “Travel, and more education.”
