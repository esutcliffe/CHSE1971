Maureen Ann (Boylan) Kopsick

Maureen Boylan grew up in Cranston. At Cranston East she belonged to the Ski Club for three years and the Hockey Club as a junior and senior. She worked on the Thunderbolt advertising staff as a junior and was a Thunderbolt alternate as a senior, and she took Office Practice in her senior year. Class records list her married name as Kopsick. She is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach Maureen, please let us know.
