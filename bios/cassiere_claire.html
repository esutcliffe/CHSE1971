Claire Marion (Cassiere) Flynn, Class of 1971

Claire Marion Cassiere grew up in Cranston, the only child of Nicholas T. and Cecile (Toracinta) Cassiere.

As a junior at Cranston High School East, in the school year that brought the very first Earth Day, Claire belonged to the Ecology Action Movement and the Folk Workshop.

After graduation, Claire went to work for the State of Rhode Island. She was still working for the state on September 2, 1978, when she married Michael Flynn of Pawtucket at St. Mary's Church in Cranston. The celebration moved on to a reception at the El Marocco Supper Club in Johnston, and the newlyweds settled in Pawtucket, which is where Claire made her home for years to come.

Claire is on our list of missing classmates. If you know how to reach her, please let us know.
