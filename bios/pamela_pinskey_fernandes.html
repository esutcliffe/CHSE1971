Pamela Michelle Pinskey Fernandes

She appears throughout reunion galleries with husband John (JP) Fernandes and on helper lists through the 40th. September 2009 class notes celebrate their 33rd anniversary, twenty-seven years owning and operating Sea Side Nursery School in Bristol, herbal study toward healing work, online Goddess Studies / ministry status, and soprano solos with the Greater Tiverton Community Chorus.
