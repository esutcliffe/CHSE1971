Robert Edward Mills

Robert Edward Mills graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. He grew up on Ruby Street in Cranston. In high school he was on the Art Service Council as a junior.

Classmates have still been looking for him; his current whereabouts are unknown. He is not listed among deceased classmates. He is not the same person as classmate Robert Dana Mills.

Living track; class card kept as missing. Not Robert Dana Mills.
