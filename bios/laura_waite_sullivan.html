Laura Jane (Waite) Sullivan

Laura Jane Waite — now Laura Waite Sullivan — graduated from Cranston High School East with the Class of 1971. She grew up on Shaw Avenue in Cranston’s Edgewood section. In high school she was Thunderbolt (Junior Editor, then Senior Editor), Chess Club, Mixed Chorus, Cranston Follies, and Library Aide.

She had a long early career at Old Stone Bank, then more than thirteen years in Brown University Advancement / Annual Giving as Administrative Coordinator.

She and her husband Michael Sullivan make their home in East Greenwich. Her mother, Marian J. (Tingley) Waite, died 3 September 2016. In recent years Laura has been active at St. Luke’s Episcopal Church in East Greenwich — Episcopal Charities contact, Women of Grace facilitator and programming committee, RSCM teaching with the St. Cecilia Choir, and service on the 2025 Rector Search Team. Michael has served as parish Treasurer.
