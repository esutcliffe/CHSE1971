Thomas Jude “Tom” Iacono

Tom Iacono was both a singer and an athlete at East. As a senior he was vice-president of the A Cappella Choir, and he also sang in the Mixed Chorus and played soccer and basketball. He served on the Class Council, tutored, and belonged to the Spanish Club, the Cervantes Honor Society and the Chess Club.

After some years in Texas in the 1980s, Tom settled in Narragansett with his wife, our classmate Nancy (Goldis) Iacono. For a time the couple welcomed guests at their Best Kept Secret B&B. In 2012 Tom wrote an article for the Providence Journal about Capt. Albert Martin, Rhode Island’s lone defender at the Alamo. Later that year Martin was inducted into the Rhode Island Heritage Hall of Fame. Tom was told his article had influenced the board’s decision, and he, Nancy and their son Ellis attended the induction. Tom is a regular at class reunions.
